The bolt-action sniper rifle operates at ranges where everything else in the arsenal is irrelevant. **The subject of the shot does not have a response available.** By the time the sound arrives the situation is already resolved.

It requires a position. High ground, a clear line, time to set up. The [Grassrivers](/gameplay/locations/grassrivers) provides all three along the water margins. [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) rooftops provide elevation. The [Keys](/gameplay/locations/leonida-keys) provide open water distances that have no parallel elsewhere in Leonida.

**The limitation is the bolt cycle.** One round, reload, one round. Against a single target at extreme range this is the correct weapon. Against multiple targets at close range it is the most dangerous thing in the hands of the operator: slow, loud after the first shot, and requiring complete exposure to reload.
