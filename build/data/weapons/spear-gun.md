The spear gun is the correct weapon for approximately five per cent of situations in Leonida. It is also the only correct weapon for those situations. **Underwater operations, approaching a target from below the surface, clearing a vessel from the waterline.** Nothing else in the arsenal functions in that environment.

The [Leonida Keys](/gameplay/locations/leonida-keys) and the [Grassrivers](/gameplay/locations/grassrivers) are where it becomes relevant. Open water approaches to boat operations, submerged entry to coastal structures, taking out people in shallow water environments.

**Above the waterline its utility drops significantly.** One shot, manual reload, no rate of fire advantage. As a surface weapon it is the choice of someone who did not have time to switch before leaving the water.
