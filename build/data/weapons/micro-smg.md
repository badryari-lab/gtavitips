The Micro SMG is purpose-built for the environment where it performs well. Drive-by sequences, vehicle-to-vehicle exchanges, confined corridor situations. **It was not designed for accuracy at distance and it does not pretend otherwise.**

The compact profile is the selling point. No other weapon in the sidearm and SMG category fits through a car window with the barrel still functional. That one specific capability has made it standard across [Vice County](/gameplay/locations/vice-county) street operations for years.

**Outside its operational envelope it becomes a liability.** At distances where a carbine is effective, the Micro SMG is wasting ammunition and providing little return. The operators who use it know this and plan accordingly.
