The golf club is [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) in a single object. Wealthy coastal residents own them. Country clubs stock them. The pro shops near the marina sell them to anyone who walks in. **There is no version of Leonida where this item raises suspicion in transit.**

The long shaft means more swing distance, which matters if the situation involves space to move. In a confined environment it becomes a liability as the arc catches walls and doorframes before it catches anything else.

The carry-through on a direct swing is significant. **The follow-through after contact is the part that ends the situation.** It is not a weapon that allows for calibrated application of force. It is committed or it is not used.
