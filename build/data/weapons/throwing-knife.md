The throwing knife occupies a specific position in the arsenal: it is the ranged option when firearms cannot be used. No muzzle report, no casing ejection, no sound that carries. **The blade arrives before the sound of anything does, because there is no sound.**

The accuracy requirement is the limiting factor. Unlike a firearm, the throw is variable based on distance, angle, and the specific physics of the blade. Inside its effective range against a stationary target the result is reliable. Against a moving target at the edge of range, it is a skill-dependent outcome.

**The recovery option is real.** A blade embedded in a surface or a target can be retrieved and re-used. In an extended operation where noise discipline is the priority and ammunition resupply is not available, that matters.
