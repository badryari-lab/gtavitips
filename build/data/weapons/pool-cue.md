The pool cue is a conditional weapon. The first strike is effective. The second strike is effective. **The third strike is the moment the cue breaks and the fight changes character.**

In [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) bars it is always within reach of whoever needs it. The bar culture across [Vice County](/gameplay/locations/vice-county) means these show up in brawl footage the way firearms show up in heist footage: reliably and in quantity.

Against a single opponent in a bar environment with room to swing it lands consistently. Against multiple opponents or anyone who can close the distance before the swing completes, **its value drops to zero faster than any other melee option in the arsenal.**
