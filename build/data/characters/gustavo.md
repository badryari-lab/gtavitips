Gustavo works the door at the [MegaMundo](/gameplay/brands/megamundo) headquarters opening, part of the security operation [Petra Navarro](/gameplay/characters/petra-navarro) has running for the event. His job is screening: everyone who comes through gets checked, no carve-outs for who they're there to protect.

**That policy catches [Lucia](/gameplay/characters/lucia-caminos) on the way in.** She's on the hired security detail for the night, brought in specifically to protect [Andres de Leon](/gameplay/characters/andres-de-leon), and Gustavo takes her guns at the door anyway. It's a small, dryly comic beat before the night turns serious: the security is unarmed by the security, right before the building comes under attack.

Beyond the door and the confiscation, Gustavo's role in the scene is limited. He's a working part of MegaMundo's event operation, not a central figure in what follows once the shooting starts.
