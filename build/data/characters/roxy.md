Roxy and [Bae-Lux](/gameplay/characters/bae-lux) built [Real Dimez](/gameplay/gangs-and-factions/real-dimez) together out of street-level [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city). The brand runs on viral rap, a heavy social media operation, and the kind of authenticity that Vice City audiences can tell is real or manufactured within thirty seconds of listening. **Roxy is the music half of that equation.**

She started with an artist named [DWNPLY](/gameplay/characters/dwnply) before navigating through several years of the Vice City music industry being exactly as difficult as the Vice City music industry always is. The signing to Only Raw Records is the current chapter. [Dre'Quan Priest](/gameplay/characters/dre-quan-priest) is the manager. The label has the infrastructure she needs.

**The goal is back at the top of the Vice City scene.** The viral presence is already there. The label deal is done. The rest of it is execution, which is the part of the music business that separates the people who make it from the people who were close.
