---
Leonida reads. Fishing mags on a workbench, a car rag in a club hallway, pulp on a motel counter, comics on a corner store rack. Pick one up, flip through it and it lands in your collection under Progress > Collections > Exploration > Magazines, Books & Comics.

Each title counts once, and most have more than one copy around the state. That matters, because some of the easy ones sit in places the story takes away from you: Jason's house in Chapter 1, the Starlet Motel in Chapter 2, the Bayside apartment in Chapter 3. Grab them while you are there and you save yourself the hunt for the backup later.

The 100% line counts these together with the seven [Book Swaps](/gameplay/collectibles/book-swaps), 30 of 34 between them. The menu only gives you a number, so keep track of what you have read as you go.
