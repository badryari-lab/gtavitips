---
Somebody in Leonida still trusts strangers with their books. Little libraries stand on front lawns from the Keys up to Ambrosia, each one a painted box with a single title inside. Walk up, open it and read. That is the whole job.

Each swap counts once. You have nothing to donate, so you take the book out, flip through it and put it back. The count lands under Progress > Collections > Exploration > Book Swaps, and it feeds the same 100% line as the magazines and comics you pick up around the state.

The catch is the tracking. The menu tells you how many you have, never which ones, so keep your own list as you work the four counties.
