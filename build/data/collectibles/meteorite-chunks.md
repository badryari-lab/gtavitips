Something came down on Mount Kalaga. The crater is still smoking next to a campsite that will not be hosting anyone again, and the rock in the middle of it glows. Walk up, take the prompt and it is yours.

That is the big one. Smaller chunks are scattered all over Leonida: on beaches, under piers, on tiny islands off the coast, inside the odd house and down on the sea floor. They glow purple in the dark, so the night shift is when you find them.

The game gives you no total and no checklist for these. The only tally is on Kenton's page under Progress > Contacts, because he is the one buying. Pawn shops that take collectibles pay too. Pick them up when you see them and sell wherever is closest.
