WAiNK is the app on your phone that wants to know what you are looking at. Point it at the right thing and it logs the place: a lighthouse, a stadium, a giant blimp, a silo painted like a cow. Leonida has 108 of them.

They come in five types. Landmarks are the big sights you can see from a mile off. Curiosities are the odd stuff: wrecks, strange houses, things that should not be where they are. Sculptures are statues and art installations. Then there are four Peewees billboards over the highways and the No Flusher tags painted on the sidewalks of Vice Beach.

When one is close, the controller buzzes and a note appears in the corner. Raise the phone, find the starburst and scan. Everything you catch lands in Progress > Collections > WAiNK Locations, and 100 of the 108 get you the trophy and the line on the 100% list.
