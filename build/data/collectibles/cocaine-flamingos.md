Leonida likes its lawn flamingos, and somebody figured out they make good hiding spots. A few of the pink plastic birds in yards, on docks and outside motels carry a package of cocaine inside. Shoot one in the body and it bursts open.

The tell is the beak. Black and white means product inside. Black and yellow means somebody just likes flamingos, and those will not even crack. The collection only shows up under Progress > Collections > Exploration once you have broken your first one.

The package is yours to sell at a fence, but the break is what counts. Hit 50 and the Inflatable Flamingo cheat unlocks for after the story. Hit 100 and the line on the 100% list is done.
