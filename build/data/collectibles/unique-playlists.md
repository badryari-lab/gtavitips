Not every station in Vice City is on the dial when you land. Two playlists stay locked until you scan the right posters, and all five of them hang inside the city: three in La Perle shops, one up in a Crosstown recording booth and one by the door of The Vault downtown.

Pull out the phone, open WAiNK and scan. Four posters together open The Sniper of La Perle, which you find under On Demand. The single red poster at The Vault adds The Vault as a full radio station. Both play in the car and through headphones on foot.

The menu counts playlists, not posters, so it never tells you which poster you missed. Clear the La Perle three in one trip and you only have two more stops.
