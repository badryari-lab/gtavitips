An artist who signs as Ahol Sniffs Glue has been spray-painting Vice City's junk. Dumped mattresses, dead fridges, traffic cones, pallets, busted TVs: if somebody left it on the curb, Ahol got to it first. The series is called Geographies of Trash, and there are 112 pieces of it.

You collect them with WAiNK. Get close, wait for the buzz, raise the phone and scan the painted object. Once it is in your collection, Vice City does the rest: people show up and haul the piece off as art. Each one is named after what it is and a number, like Mattress 72 or Cone 12.

None of it counts toward 100% or toward the 100 WAiNK scans. This one is for the completionists and anyone who likes walking alleys. Start in Vice Beach, where Ahol did a third of the work.
