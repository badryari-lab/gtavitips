---
Kenton Rees runs dives out of Key Lento and he has a map full of old Spanish ships. After your first trip out with him in Chapter 1, he starts marking wrecks as purple chests on your map. Take a boat with scuba gear, drop over the circle, find the hull and sweep it with the metal detector until every piece is up.

Each wreck counts as its own set. Clear a few, go back to his shop and he calls with the next ones. What you bring up sells to him for cash, one piece at a time or a full haul. The wrecks also tick off Underwater Discoveries, which is the bigger list: 33 spots across the sea floor, from galleons to boats and cars that never made it home.

Gold doubloons are the loose change of the job. The first wreck is full of them, and more lie scattered on the seabed. The game counts the ones you sell to Kenton, up to 99, but nobody has a number for how many are down there. If the detector pings, dig.
