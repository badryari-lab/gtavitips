Somebody signing as 1010 has been painting Vice City, and they want you to see it from the right angle. The challenge turns up on Snapmatic. It points you at five murals, each with a title that reads like a complaint letter to the people who run the city.

Get to the mark 1010 left, line up the mural and capture it with your phone. Each one lands in its own collection under Progress > Collections > 1010 Murals. Four sit close together on the mainland, two of them in Shoreline. The fifth is across the water in Washington Beach.

None of it counts toward 100%. You do it for the car: all five murals unlock a '95 Grotti Cheetah in a paint job you will not find anywhere else.
