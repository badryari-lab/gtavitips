Leonida dresses itself out of magazines. Style mags lie around in bedrooms, back offices and on rooftops, and each one carries a look: a haircut, a makeup style, a beard, a page of tattoo flash. Point WAiNK at the cover and the look is yours to buy at a salon or a tattoo studio.

The game splits them four ways. Jason has 13 magazine styles and Lucia has 15, and some only unlock for the character they were made for. The 17 magazine tattoos work for either. The last group is 34 luxury watches, real pickups with a gold icon, mostly sitting with the valuables at gang operations. Each one goes straight into the wardrobe.

All of it lands in Progress > Collections > Discovered Styles, and 70 of 79 clear the 100% line. The watches pay extra: ten unlock the Move Time Forward cheat, twenty unlock Pause Time of Day, both for after the story.
