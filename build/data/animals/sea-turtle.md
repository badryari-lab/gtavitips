Sea turtles drift over the coral of the [Leonida Keys](/gameplay/locations/leonida-keys). **Dive the reefs and they are there, slow and indifferent, with the reef fish moving around them.**

It is a cheap day for the animal log. The same reefs hold moray [eels](/gameplay/animals/eel) and lionfish, so one dive can fill several entries under Progress > Animals. Gear and spots are under [Diving](/gameplay/activities/diving).
