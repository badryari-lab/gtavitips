The southern leopard frog is the ambient sound species of Leonida's wetlands. Individually it is a small spotted frog, unremarkable in size, that hops from vegetation to water and back. **In groups at night, near still water, the collective call is the sound that defines the [Grassrivers](/gameplay/locations/grassrivers) after dark.**

Real Florida wetlands have this quality: at night the frog calls overlap into a continuous ambient sound that is as much a feature of the environment as the water itself. Leonida's Grassrivers will have this too. It is not a detail that requires a trailer sighting. It is a requirement for the environment to feel correct.

The player does not interact with frogs meaningfully. They scatter on approach. They are the background audio of every wetland scene and the visual detail at the waterline that confirms the ecosystem is functioning correctly.
