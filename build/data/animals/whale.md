The whale in Leonida is not a static model at depth. It operates at surface and near-surface depth, visible from boats in open water.

Humpback whales migrate along the US Atlantic coast. Right whales, critically endangered, are present in Florida waters seasonally. **The Leonida whale is large enough that seeing it from a boat changes the scale reference of everything else in the shot.**

It is not dangerous. It does not interact with the player in a damaging way. It is there for the same reason the clouds are there: the game world communicates its scale through objects that are larger than anything else currently in frame. **The whale is that object for the ocean.**
