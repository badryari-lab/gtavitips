The [alligator](/gameplay/animals/american-alligator) keeps to fresh water. **The American crocodile works the coast.** Look along the [Grassrivers](/gameplay/locations/grassrivers) shore, on the small islands and in the water near the sea.

Take it with a scoped Shrewsbury SM-69. One shot to the head or heart keeps the carcass pristine, and Bret buys Pristine or Good only. More under [Hunting](/gameplay/activities/hunting). Bret's sixth Target Deck wants one, next to a Leonida Cottonmouth and a Marsh Rabbit.

**The Unique Crocodile lies between Wetman Bay and Cottonmouth Lake.** It counts toward the Legendary Hunter challenge.

Not hunting? Raise the binoculars or a rifle scope, zoom in until Study shows, then hold R1 (RB). It goes into the animal log under Progress > Animals.
