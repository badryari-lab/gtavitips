**Nutria live in Buckskin Swamp, and Bret wants one.** His third Target Deck, Cane Toad Venom Glands, marks West Buckskin Swamp and asks for a nutria next to a [Cane Toad](/gameplay/animals/cane-toad) and a [Nile Monitor](/gameplay/animals/nile-monitor).

Use the Wortham W-22, the small-game rifle. One shot to the head or heart keeps the carcass pristine, and Bret buys Pristine or Good only. More under [Hunting](/gameplay/activities/hunting). Keep an eye on the water. [Alligators](/gameplay/animals/american-alligator) live there too.

Not hunting? Raise the binoculars or a rifle scope, zoom in until Study shows, then hold R1 (RB). It goes into the animal log under Progress > Animals.
