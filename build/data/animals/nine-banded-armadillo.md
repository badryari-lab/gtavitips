Bret's fourth Target Deck is called Rattlesnake Venom and an Armadillo Shell, and **the shell is this one.** He marks the Kelly River for it, next to a Golden [Fox](/gameplay/animals/fox) and an Eastern Diamondback Rattlesnake.

Use the Wortham W-22, the small-game rifle from Ammu-Nation's Hunting stores. One shot to the head or heart keeps the carcass pristine, and Bret buys Pristine or Good only. More under [Hunting](/gameplay/activities/hunting).

Not hunting? Raise the binoculars or a rifle scope, zoom in until Study shows, then hold R1 (RB). It goes into the animal log under Progress > Animals.
