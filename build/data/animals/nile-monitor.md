Nile monitors keep to Buckskin Swamp. **Bret's third Target Deck wants one**, next to a [Cane Toad](/gameplay/animals/cane-toad) and a [Nutria](/gameplay/animals/nutria), with West Buckskin Swamp on the map.

Use the Wortham W-22, the small-game rifle. One shot to the head or heart keeps the carcass pristine, and Bret buys Pristine or Good only. More under [Hunting](/gameplay/activities/hunting).

Not hunting? Raise the binoculars or a rifle scope, zoom in until Study shows, then hold R1 (RB). It goes into the animal log under Progress > Animals.
