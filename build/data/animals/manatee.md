West Indian manatees are Florida's most recognisable marine conservation subject. They are large, slow, warm-water dependent, and have no natural predators other than boat propellers. In real Florida, boat speed limits in manatee zones are enforced.

In Leonida, the manatee tests how seriously the wildlife protection laws are enforced. **Harming a manatee earns notoriety and a law enforcement response from whatever agency monitors the waterways.** Previous games in this setting have done versions of this with protected species.

They surface to breathe, they browse on aquatic vegetation, they move slowly in groups through warm inshore channels. The [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) marina and the river mouths near Port Gellhorn are the expected habitat. **In visual terms they are one of the largest animals in the inshore environment, which makes them impossible to miss when present.**
