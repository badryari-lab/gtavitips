Spectacled caimans keep to the wetlands. **Bret's twelfth Target Deck wants the albino.** It sits next to a Northern Silver [Coyote](/gameplay/animals/coyote) and a Soot-Spotted Cottontail Rabbit on the same list.

Take it with a scoped Shrewsbury SM-69. One shot to the head or heart keeps the carcass pristine, and Bret buys Pristine or Good only. More under [Hunting](/gameplay/activities/hunting).

Not hunting? Raise the binoculars or a rifle scope, zoom in until Study shows, then hold R1 (RB). It goes into the animal log under Progress > Animals.
