Bobcats keep to forest and wetland. **The one Bret wants is black.** His ninth Target Deck asks for a Leonida Black Bobcat, next to a Blue Axanthic [Iguana](/gameplay/animals/iguana) and a Gulf Coast Box Turtle.

Bring the Hawk & Little Mediator, the rifle the game recommends for bobcats. One shot to the head or heart keeps the carcass pristine, and Bret buys Pristine or Good only. More under [Hunting](/gameplay/activities/hunting).

Not hunting? Raise the binoculars or a rifle scope, zoom in until Study shows, then hold R1 (RB). It goes into the animal log under Progress > Animals.
