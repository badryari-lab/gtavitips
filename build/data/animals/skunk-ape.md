The Skunk Ape is no rumour. **Drive south of [Hamlet](/gameplay/locations/hamlet) to Cottonmouth Lake and take the wooded dirt road along its south-east shore.** At the end of it something big and hairy is eating a deer.

It bolts the moment you get close. Do not bother shooting, and you cannot catch it. Listen for the growl and use Focus (L3 + R3) to keep its direction, then follow the trail into the woods.

The trail ends at a human-shaped effigy tied to a tree. Get close and look it over, and Jason goes down: a short haze, a glimpse of enormous hairy feet, and he wakes up nearby. That is the whole encounter, and it is open from Chapter 1.

There is more for anyone who wants it. Kirk, a local who calls the creature Mr. Mangrove, posts "Have You Seen Skunk Ape???" flyers; the easiest is at Skeeter's bar in Hamlet. Read it, call the number, and his side jobs start, some of them only at night.
