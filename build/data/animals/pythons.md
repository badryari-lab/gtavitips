The Burmese python is the real-world Florida Everglades' most well-documented ecological disaster. Escaped and released pets established a breeding population in the late twentieth century. They are now one of the dominant predators of the south Florida wetlands, having consumed most of the small and medium mammal population.

Leonida's [Grassrivers](/gameplay/locations/grassrivers) reflects this. **The pythons are there. They are large.** A fully grown Burmese python reaches lengths that make most encounters unexpectedly significant. They are not fast in the conventional sense, but they do not need to be.

The attack is a constriction sequence rather than a direct strike. Getting into range is enough. **Breaking the constriction requires sustained input.** In water the combination of a python and an alligator in the same zone creates an environment that punishes carelessness with consistency.
