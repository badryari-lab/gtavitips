Coyotes are not the prize. **The colours are.** Bret's fifth Target Deck asks for a Northern Black Coyote, next to a Black [Fox](/gameplay/animals/fox) and a Leonida Black [Panther](/gameplay/animals/florida-panther). His twelfth wants a Northern Silver Coyote. Both are rare.

Bring the Hawk & Little Mediator, the rifle the game recommends for coyotes. One shot to the head or heart keeps the carcass pristine, and Bret buys Pristine or Good only. More under [Hunting](/gameplay/activities/hunting).

One coyote has a reputation. **The Unique Coyote raids the farms north of Belville**, and it counts toward the Legendary Hunter challenge.

Not hunting? Raise the binoculars or a rifle scope, zoom in until Study shows, then hold R1 (RB). It goes into the animal log under Progress > Animals.
