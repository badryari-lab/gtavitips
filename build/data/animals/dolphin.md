Dolphins run the coastal water. **Take a boat out past the shore and they find you, riding the wake and breaking the surface beside the hull.**

They are an entry in the animal log, and an awkward one. A dolphin is only up for a moment. Keep the binoculars on the spot where it jumped and hold R1 (RB) the instant Study appears.

Nothing hunts them here. They are a sighting, not a target.
