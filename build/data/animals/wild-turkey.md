Bret's second Target Deck wants a turkey, but not any turkey. **It has to be the Leonida Wild White Tom, white feathers and all.** A brown one does not count. Look around Adler Creek, [Bonanza](/gameplay/locations/bonanza) and [Mount Kalaga National Park](/gameplay/locations/mount-kalaga-national-park), the ground he marks for that deck.

Use the Wortham W-22, the small-game rifle. One shot to the head or heart keeps the carcass pristine, and Bret buys Pristine or Good only. More under [Hunting](/gameplay/activities/hunting). The same deck asks for an Eastern Spotted [Skunk](/gameplay/animals/skunk) and a [wild boar](/gameplay/animals/boar).

Not hunting? Raise the binoculars or a rifle scope, zoom in until Study shows, then hold R1 (RB). It goes into the animal log under Progress > Animals.
