Weed growing in GTA 6 sits at the production end of Leonida's drug economy. The state's climate makes outdoor cultivation viable across much of [Vice County](/gameplay/locations/vice-county)'s rural interior. Indoor operations run in converted residential and commercial spaces closer to the city. Both formats appear in official footage.

**Florida's legal cannabis market has created a dual economy that GTA 6 uses directly.** Licensed dispensaries operate openly in [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) while unlicensed operations run product in parallel. The parody is built into the geography. The legal side provides cover. The illegal side provides margin.

The grow operation feeds product into the distribution network. What gets cultivated moves through fencing contacts, street dealers, and faction-controlled supply chains. Growing is the start of a chain that runs through multiple confirmed activities and factions in Leonida.

The grow operation requires setup, maintenance, and protection. Indoor setups need equipment sourced from supply contacts. Rural outdoor operations need land access and enough distance from law enforcement patrol routes. Both have harvest cycles that determine when product becomes available for distribution.

**Rival operations and law enforcement create pressure on active grow sites.** An unprotected indoor grow in [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) is a target for rivals looking to take the supply. A rural outdoor operation draws attention if traffic patterns change. Managing the operation is as much about logistics and territory as it is about the grow itself.

Product quality affects street value. The grade of what gets cultivated determines the price point at which it moves through the distribution chain. Higher quality takes longer and costs more to produce. The margin calculation is part of the activity's economy.
