[Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) has a car culture the way Miami has a car culture, loud, visible, and taken seriously. Custom builds, modified suspensions, specific wheel sizes that mean something to the people who know. Car meets are where that culture surfaces publicly. **You bring what you built. Everyone else brings what they built.** The difference between them is the conversation.

The Trailer 1 car meet shot shows custom vehicles rolling in after dark. A pink car entering with a sticker in the back right window reading **"27IN"**, 27-inch rims. The kind of detail that goes unnoticed unless you are already inside the culture. Rockstar put it there for the people who would catch it.

Car meets are the civilian side of a scene that also includes [illegal street racing](/gameplay/activities/racing) and [street takeovers](/gameplay/activities/street-takeovers). They are connected but distinct. The meet is the social event. What happens after the meet is a different activity.

Car meets function as a social hub for Vice City's automotive scene. Attending with a customized vehicle affects standing within the scene, **what you show up in matters.** The [High Rollerz](/gameplay/gangs-and-factions/high-rollerz) crew operates in this space, and reputation built at meets connects to their operations and the racing circuit they run.

Whether meets include judged competitions, timed events, or purely social interaction is not fully established in official material. What is confirmed: custom builds are the currency, and the [vehicle customization system](/gameplay/activities/vehicle-customization) feeds directly into how you show up.
