Nobody in Leonida plays snooker. **There is no snooker table in the state.** No twelve-foot cloth, no fifteen reds, no break building. Every cue in every bar is there for pool.

That is where the game is. Bars across Leonida keep a table in the back, and seven regulars hang around them waiting for someone with cash. Pay in before the break, take double back if you win. The pot rarely tops $20. The solo tables are free and pay nothing.

**Beat five of the regulars and it counts toward 100%.** One of them drinks at [Delights](/gameplay/locations/delights-port-gellhorn) in Port Gellhorn. The others are spread over bars elsewhere in the state, and none of them is in a hurry. More in [games](/gameplay/activities/games) and [gambling](/gameplay/activities/gambling).

Source: GTA VI, in-game.
