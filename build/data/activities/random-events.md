Nothing on the map warns you. **Random events are the scenes Leonida stages on its own, out on the road and in the swamp.** A scream, a gunshot, a siren where there shouldn't be one. Hold Focus and the camera points you at the source. Finish one and it is gone for good. Drive past and it comes back later. The rarest happen once.

**The Skunk Ape.** Take the road along Cottonmouth Lake in Mariana County and listen for a shriek. Something big and hairy stands over a carcass in the road, then crashes off into the marsh. Follow it and it vanishes. Further in the woods hangs a carved figure tied to a tree, and lingering beside it ends badly for your head. It is open from Chapter 1, it never goes away, and it opens an optional side story. The [Skunk Ape](/gameplay/animals/skunk-ape) has its own page.

**The UFO.** The one event with a trophy. Head to the dirt road east of Port Gellhorn, near the [Starlet Motel](/gameplay/locations/starlet-motel), after 2am and alone. Look up. The Truth Is Out There pops when it shows.

**The repo man.** Around one in the afternoon on NE 3rd Street in Vice City, an owner squares up to a tow truck driver over a Pegassi Tempesta. Settle it your way and the car is yours to take. It is on Randy's Wish List from Chapter 3.

**The polar bear truck.** On the road to the zoo, protesters block a truck hauling a polar bear. There is nothing to win. You will read about it in the news afterwards.
