Hunting in Leonida runs through one man. **Bret Vogel, Jason's old army friend, runs a hunting business out of a cabin in the woods near [Mount Kalaga](/gameplay/locations/mount-kalaga-national-park).** Once the story has moved on a little, he texts Jason. Lucia never gets the message.

The first hunt is the lesson. Bret hands over the Killtelligence SM-69, a scoped rifle with his company's name on it, and takes you after a deer. Focus (L3 and R3) shows the scent trail, white at first and yellow as you close in. Study the animal through the scope or binoculars before you shoot: the card names the right weapon and what the carcass is worth. Lose the rifle later and an Ammu-Nation weapon locker has it.

**Then come the orders.** Bret posts fifteen Target Decks, one at a time: finish a deck and he texts the next. Each names two or three animals and marks their ground on the map, under Map > Activities > Target Deck. All fifteen count toward 100%, and they stay open after the story.

The first deck wants a [whitetail](/gameplay/animals/white-tailed-deer) buck and doe, out in [Mount Kalaga National Park](/gameplay/locations/mount-kalaga-national-park) and around the Abandoned Concrete Plant. The second gets pickier: a Leonida Wild White Tom (white feathers only, a brown turkey does not count), an Eastern Spotted [Skunk](/gameplay/animals/skunk) that shows up after about 8 PM, and a [wild boar](/gameplay/animals/boar). That deck's ground is Adler Creek, [Bonanza](/gameplay/locations/bonanza) and Mount Kalaga.

Deck three moves into the swamp: a Cane Toad, a Nutria and a Nile Monitor, with West Buckskin Swamp on the map. Deck four, Rattlesnake Venom and an Armadillo Shell, sends you to the Kelly River for a Golden Fox, an Eastern Diamondback Rattlesnake and a Nine-Banded Armadillo. **The fox has to be the golden one.** A plain red [fox](/gameplay/animals/fox) is just a fox.

Deck five wants black fur: a Leonida Black [Panther](/gameplay/animals/florida-panther), a Black Fox and a Northern Black Coyote. **After that the orders only get rarer.** Deck six: a Leonida Cottonmouth, an American Crocodile and a Marsh Rabbit. Deck seven: a Canada Goose, a North American White River Otter and a Northern Albino [Raccoon](/gameplay/animals/raccoon). Deck eight: an American [Black Bear](/gameplay/animals/american-black-bear), a Gloriana [Opossum](/gameplay/animals/opossum) and a Spotted Wild Boar. Deck nine: a Blue Axanthic [Iguana](/gameplay/animals/iguana), a Gulf Coast Box Turtle and a Leonida Black Bobcat. Deck ten: an Albino American [Alligator](/gameplay/animals/american-alligator), a Round-Tailed Muskrat and a Mosaic Mottled [Duck](/gameplay/animals/duck). Deck eleven: a Super Red Iguana, a Piebald Whitetail Buck and a Softshell Turtle. Deck twelve: an Albino Spectacled Caiman, a Northern Silver Coyote and a Soot-Spotted Cottontail Rabbit.

**Then there are the ones with a reputation.** Now and then Bret texts a Snapmatic of a unique animal and roughly where it was seen. The Unique Buck works the coast northeast of Grayson. The Unique Panther hides on Belpoint Island, west of [Capri](/gameplay/locations/capri). The Unique Crocodile lies between Wetman Bay and Cottonmouth Lake, and the Unique Swamp Alligator goes deep into Buckskin Swamp. The [Unique Black Bear](/gameplay/animals/american-black-bear) roams south of [Hamlet](/gameplay/locations/hamlet), the Unique Wild Boar keeps to the old radar station at Wild Hog Point, the Unique Coyote raids the farms north of Belville, and the Unique White Fox hangs around Howe Dairy in north Emerald Springs. Take every unique animal down and the Legendary Hunter challenge is yours, under Progress > Challenges > Survival.

One shot to the head or heart keeps a carcass pristine. Every extra round knocks the grade down, and Bret takes only Pristine or Good. Small game rides back in your vehicle to his processing area. [Bears](/gameplay/animals/american-black-bear) and [alligators](/gameplay/animals/american-alligator) do not get lifted: send Bret a photo and the location, and he collects. For small game, Ammu-Nation's Hunting stores are the only place that sells the Wortham W-22.

Source: GTA VI, in-game.
