Combat training connects directly to the fighting capability system. The protagonist enters a training facility, works with trainers or equipment, and develops specific skills that carry into both the cage fighting circuit and the open-world confrontations that [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) generates constantly.

The gym system returns in Vice City: MMA gyms, boxing facilities, and the martial arts training spaces that a city with Vice City's energy and its fighting circuit generates. **Training is preparation. Vice City provides the occasions to use it.**

Each training type develops specific combat attributes. Boxing training improves strike power and accuracy. Grappling develops takedown and submission capability relevant to cage fighting. Weapons training applies to the broader combat skill set. The combination determines how the protagonist performs across the full range of physical confrontations.

**The training facility is also a social location.** Trainers and regular attendees at combat gyms in GTA cities have always had connections to both legitimate and criminal networks. The person who trains you and the people who train alongside you are part of the [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) social map.
