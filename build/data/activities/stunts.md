Leonida rewards anyone willing to leave the ground. **Four kinds of stunt, 397 in all, each with its own name and its own spot on the map.** Nobody asks for all of them. Any 250 unlock Cunning Stunts, and the 100% checklist wants 300.

**Stunt Jumps (130).** Ramps, dirt mounds, rooftops of parking garages. Hit one fast and the camera goes cinematic while you hang in the air. The left stick still steers. Most of them sit in Vice-Dale County. Every jump carries a challenge on top: a minimum speed, a set distance, a specific vehicle, or the police on your tail with a wanted level. Land it once and it counts. The challenge is for bragging rights.

**Under the Bridge (202).** The biggest pile and the fastest to clear. Take a plane or a helicopter and thread it beneath a bridge or an overpass.

**Knife Flights (40).** Roll a plane onto its side, ninety degrees, and push it through the gap between two towers. Most of the gaps are in [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city).

**BASE Jumps (25).** Mostly helipads and roof ledges on the tallest towers in the city, plus cliffs in [Mount Kalaga National Park](/gameplay/locations/mount-kalaga-national-park). Jump, open the chute, drop through the rings and beat the par time. Bring a parachute: [Ammu-Nation](/faq/where-to-buy-weapons-in-gta-6) sells them.

To find them, open the map index, go to Exploration and pick Stunt Spots. The count by kind and by region lives under Progress, then Activities, then Stunts.

Source: GTA VI, in-game.
