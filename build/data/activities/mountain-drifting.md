Leonida has no drift scene to sign up for. **No drift races, no drift score, no judges on the hillside.** What it has is [Mount Kalaga](/gameplay/locations/mount-kalaga) in Lummox County, the highest ground in the state, and a road down it that rewards anyone who can hold a slide.

Slide as long as you like. The game counts nothing for it, so the only reason is the run itself.

**The timed run on the mountain is on two wheels.** The Kalaga Cycling Trial starts at the summit and ends at the foot, against the clock. Gold takes 1:05, and it is one of the race challenge golds that count toward 100%.

For engines, the off-road series Animal Crackers runs in Lummox and Mariana County, and the Thrillbilly Mud Club has a Hill Climb Trial of its own. More in [racing](/gameplay/activities/racing).

Source: GTA VI, in-game.
