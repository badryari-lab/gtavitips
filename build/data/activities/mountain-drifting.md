Mountain drifting uses the elevation change that Mount Kalaga and the [Vice County](/gameplay/locations/vice-county) interior provide. The descent roads from the mountain create the gradient that makes drifting on loose surface possible at speeds not available on flat terrain. **The activity is the driving discipline that the mountain's geometry was designed to enable.**

The mountain-based driving format is established. Mount Kalaga provides a different but equivalent geography: Florida terrain adapted for GTA's creative needs, with descent roads and surface types that support the drifting activity.

The mechanic manages throttle, steering, and handbrake inputs across a surface that behaves differently from tarmac. Loose gravel and dirt surfaces reduce grip predictably. Gradient changes the weight transfer timing. **A controlled drift on a mountain descent is a coordinated series of inputs rather than a single action.**

Vehicle choice is part of the activity. Off-road capable vehicles with rear-wheel-drive bias are the appropriate tool. Standard road vehicles on mountain terrain without the surface handling will lose control rather than drift. The vehicle customization activity connects directly: suspension, tires, and drivetrain configuration all affect mountain drifting performance.
