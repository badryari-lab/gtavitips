Racing in Leonida runs at several levels. **Forty races in six series**, and at each venue one race opens the next. You pay a buy-in and, if your own car does not fit, a rental. A win more than covers both.

The streets belong to Hit 'n' Run VC. The crew opens up once the story reaches [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city), posts its meet-ups by text, and races through Salton, La Perle, Ocean Beach and Gloriana Key. Out on the freeway, a carload of racers sometimes pulls alongside and asks for one on the spot.

**The tracks are out of town.** Gellhorn International Raceway, south of [Port Gellhorn](/gameplay/locations/port-gellhorn), holds the most races and starts you on an amateur series. Boone Speedway in Hamlet runs elimination races, Ambrosia Raceway runs demolition races, and the Thrillbilly Mud Club in north Kelly County runs dirt track and oval races. Animal Crackers takes off-road racing through Lummox and Mariana County.

Every car carries a rating, and every race has a rating band. Too slow and the organizer turns you away. Some races allow nitrous. Thirty different wins count toward 100%. Build the car at the [vehicle customization](/gameplay/activities/vehicle-customization) shops, then bring it.

Source: GTA VI, in-game.
