Leonida has two strip clubs, and they are not the same night out. **[Jack of Hearts](/gameplay/locations/jack-of-hearts) sits on Mirimar Drive at the north end of Crosstown in [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city).** It belongs to [Boobie Ike](/gameplay/characters/boobie-ike): big stage setups, low light and Make It Rain Mondays on the posters. The doors stay shut until the story brings you there.

[Delights Cabaret](/gameplay/locations/delights-port-gellhorn) is the other one, out in [Port Gellhorn](/gameplay/locations/port-gellhorn), a long drive from the Vice City spotlight.

Inside, you can drink, watch the stage, throw money at the dancers and talk to them. For a private dance, look at a dancer with L2, greet her and pay. She takes you to a back room. Tips during the dance are $50 a time, they keep it going, and they add up fast. **Walk in with a few hundred dollars at least.**

Both Jason and Lucia can walk in, alone or together.

Source: GTA VI, in-game.
