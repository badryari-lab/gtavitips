The tennis mechanic returns with a solid foundation: shot selection (topspin, slice, lob), court positioning, and opponent AI that adapts to the player's approach. Leonida builds on this with updated physics and the Vice City setting.

The mechanic uses a shot timing system with shot type selection. Power, placement, and spin combine to create a tennis simulation that rewards consistent play. **Opponent difficulty scales with the circuit tier**: casual club matches versus competitive players in the [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) tennis community.

The social function applies here as it does to all sport activities. A tennis match with a specific character is a social interaction that the sport provides cover for. The court is a place where conversations happen that would not happen anywhere else.
