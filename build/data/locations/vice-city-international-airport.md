The official map just calls it VCIA. Vice City International Airport fills a big block of ground on the west side of [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city): I-404 on the north side, I-97 to the east, Salton and [Little Cuba](/gameplay/locations/little-cuba) beyond that.

**Two runways, a central terminal and commercial jets parked at the gates.** It is a working airport, and it acts like one.
%%%GROUND%%%
The terminal is open. The airside is not. **Getting past the restricted gates draws the police fast.** The parked jets are the reason people try anyway.

Light aircraft are easier to find elsewhere. The airport is where the big ones sit.
