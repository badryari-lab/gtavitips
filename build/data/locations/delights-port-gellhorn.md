Delights Port Gellhorn serves the Port Gellhorn workforce. Not the [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) tourist demographic. Not the [South Beach](/gameplay/locations/south-beach) crowd. The people who work the docks and the warehouses and the logistics corridor between Port Gellhorn and the Vice County interior. **This is the venue those people go to when the shift ends.**

The name suggests a certain kind of establishment. Port towns in real Florida, and in Leonida too, have always had this category of venue: adult entertainment adjacent, cash-operating, and useful as a social environment for people who work irregular hours in physical jobs and have cash in their pockets at the end of a shift.

In Leonida, venues like Delights serve as character contact locations, information exchange environments, and the places where the working economy of a port town converges after hours. The Delights is where Port Gellhorn's social and operational threads cross.
%%%GROUND%%%
The Port Gellhorn position means the Delights operates outside the [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) surveillance infrastructure. Standard county coverage rather than tourist-zone intensity. The venue's clientele does not attract tourist-tier law enforcement attention.

**Cash operations and irregular hours are the baseline conditions.** The social function of the venue for the port workforce means it has the ambient character of a working-town establishment rather than an entertainment district club.

Access via the Port Gellhorn road network. The marshland on the town's edge provides the alternative route for arrivals that prefer the waterway.
