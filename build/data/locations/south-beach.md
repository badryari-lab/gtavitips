South Beach is the sequence that opens every piece of official footage. The one with the neon and the palm trees and the ocean in the background and the cars moving slowly along Ocean Drive because everyone wants to be seen. This is the [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) everyone pictures before they get there, and it's exactly what's waiting when you do.

The real-world South Beach is Miami Beach's southern section: the Art Deco Historic District, Ocean Drive, the promenade, the hotels with the pink and yellow facades and the neon signage. **The architecture holds up close enough that anyone who's seen the real Miami Beach clocks it immediately.**

What the postcard does not show is the infrastructure behind the scene. The promenade is beautiful. The side streets behind it are the actual operational environment: narrower, more complicated to navigate at speed, and containing the service access routes and parking infrastructure that the hotels and clubs require.
%%%GROUND%%%
South Beach is a difficult environment. The density is the problem. **Any action taken here is witnessed.** The foot traffic on the promenade at evening is continuous. The cameras on the hotel exteriors cover the street frontage. The [VCPD](/gameplay/gangs-and-factions/vcpd) patrol density in the tourist corridor is the highest in [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city).

The promenade itself is pedestrian priority. Vehicle access is constrained to Ocean Drive and the side streets. Moving at speed through the tourist strip on foot means navigating crowds. Moving at speed in a vehicle means navigating a road that is not designed for speed.

The window where South Beach becomes operationally manageable is early morning: before the promenade fills, before the tourist hotels have their foot traffic on the street, and before the VCPD shift has fully deployed. That window is short. The strip activates early.
