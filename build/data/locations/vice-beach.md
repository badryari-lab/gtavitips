Look east from [Downtown](/gameplay/locations/downtown-vice-city) and the skyline stops at the water. Past the bay sits Vice Beach: one long island facing the Atlantic Ocean, and the part of Leonida everyone puts on a postcard.

Rockstar's map names it as its own city in Vice-Dale County, not a neighbourhood of [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city). Two districts carry most of it. **[Ocean Beach](/gameplay/locations/ocean-beach) at the southern tip, [Washington Beach](/gameplay/locations/washington-beach) along the north.** Oldby Island sits tight against the western shore.

The bay between Vice Beach and the mainland is crowded with smaller islands: Leaf Links, Penmarch Isle, the Rialto Islands, Casanova, Vespucci, Starfish, Waxman and Salton Memorial. The roads over the water use them as stepping stones. Dalton Island lies further south across Catalan Bay.

The real thing is Miami Beach, and it does not try to hide it. Same island, same split between the bay and the ocean, same Art Deco on the south end.
%%%GROUND%%%
Getting on and off the island means a causeway or a boat. **Every road out runs over open water, which makes the bridges the first place anyone gets boxed in.**

The beach has its own patrol. [Vice Beach Police](/gameplay/gangs-and-factions/vice-beach-police) work the sand and the promenade, and the mainland cops are never more than a bridge away. The busy end is the south. The further north you drive, the quieter the island gets.
