Waning Sands sits on the Leonard County coast, north of [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city). I-97 runs straight through it. Vinewood Beach lies across the water to the east, and Richard Barebone State Park starts to the north.

**The mall is the headline.** The map marks the Evergreen Mall of Waning Sands on the south side of town, and its sign showed up in Rockstar's first trailer. Sabal Palms covers the northern edge.

It is the suburbs with a coastline. Shopping, traffic and a beach across the inlet.
%%%GROUND%%%
I-97 is the spine. South takes you toward Belville and the city, north toward [Millport](/gameplay/locations/millport). **Off the interstate, it is malls and side streets.**
