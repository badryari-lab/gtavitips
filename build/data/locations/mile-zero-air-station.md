The road through the Keys ends where the military starts. Past [Key Lento](/gameplay/locations/key-lento), Route 1A hops the last islands to Goose Key, and Goose Key is Mile Zero Air Station: runway, hangars, fences and people paid to keep you out.

Rockstar's map puts it at the southwest tip of [Mariana County](/gameplay/locations/mariana-county), with the small town of Southwind right next door. Beyond it there is only open water.

**Inside the wire sits the hardware nobody sells you.** Fighter jets and helicopters around the airfield. The Hauners Rhino parked near the hangars, tracks and all.
%%%GROUND%%%
The base is marked as a restricted zone, and the soldiers act like it. Get spotted and the wanted level climbs at once, with rifles already pointed your way.

**Getting in is half the job. Getting off the island is the other half.** By road there is only the bridge chain back to Key Lento. By air, whatever you took is faster than anything chasing you on the ground.
