The Monument of Leonida is the state landmark. The sign in Los Santos, the Mount Chiliad peak, the Statue of Happiness in Liberty City. **The Monument of Leonida is Leonida's equivalent.** The thing that sits on the map and tells you where you are in the state.

What the monument represents in Leonida's internal mythology is the kind of grandiose state self-image that Florida has always generated. The real Florida has the Sunshine Skyway Bridge, the Welcome to Florida signs, the scale of the Everglades. Leonida has the monument. The scale is appropriate for a state that has always believed its own press.

The monument works the way every Leonida landmark works: a navigation anchor, a magnet for anyone passing through, and the kind of thing you end up standing at the base of once, just to see it up close.
%%%GROUND%%%
Landmarks of this scale create a specific kind of mission geometry. The base of the monument and the surrounding area are the location. The monument itself is the vertical feature. The city uses both.

**The interior position away from [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) means the monument site has different law enforcement coverage from the coastal zones.** Less patrol density, more open terrain, and the landscape that makes large-scale confrontations possible without the witness density of the urban core.

The monument's a pilgrimage stop, plain and simple. People go out of their way just to stand at the base of it.
