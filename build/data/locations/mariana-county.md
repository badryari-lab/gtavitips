South of the airport and west of [Hamlet](/gameplay/locations/hamlet), the roads give up and the water takes over. Mariana County is the swamp and the islands: [Grassrivers](/gameplay/locations/grassrivers) on the mainland, the [Leonida Keys](/gameplay/locations/leonida-keys) running out to sea below it. It covers the whole southwest corner of the state.

**The mainland half is wetland.** Grassrivers is Leonida's Everglades: sawgrass, mangroves, slow channels, and the [alligators](/gameplay/animals/american-alligator) and [pythons](/gameplay/animals/pythons) that come with them. A few small places hold on around the edges. Rivers drain the whole thing into Leonida Bay and the Gulf.

**The island half is the Keys.** Route 1A leaves the mainland past Hamlet, hops the Shoeless Keys to [Key Lento](/gameplay/locations/key-lento), then runs southwest over Maroon Key and Abismo Key to Southwind and the Mile Zero Air Station at the far end of the state. The Florida Keys, done the Leonida way.

The roads are simple because there are hardly any. US 82 is the main line across the mainland, running southeast from the Nehrling River country toward Hamlet. Route 1A is the only road through the Keys. **Everything else is a boat.**
%%%GROUND%%%
**The Gulf coast.** Big Pine and Bobcat Key sit on the west coast below the Nehrling River, with Bobcat Bight opening up beside them. The Henry J Edwards Airport is just inland, a small strip in the middle of nowhere.

**Grassrivers.** [Grassrivers](/gameplay/locations/grassrivers) is the heart of the county. The Paradise RV Park sits on its edge, and Watson Bay is the town past it on the water. Pelican Valley and Cottonmouth Lake lie to the east, running down toward Leonida Bay. Out here a [kayak](/gameplay/activities/kayaking) goes places a car never will.

**The road south.** Past Hamlet the coast breaks apart into islands. Corsair Key and Peregrine Bay lie east of Route 1A, the Shoeless Keys and Gambit Bay along it.

**Key Lento.** [Key Lento](/gameplay/locations/key-lento) is the first big island and the busiest. [Brian's Boat Works & Marina](/gameplay/locations/brians-boat-works-and-marina) works out of here, and the reefs around it are good [diving](/gameplay/activities/diving) water.

**The far end.** Maroon Key, Abismo Key and the Permit Keys carry Route 1A southwest to Southwind. Goose Key and the Mile Zero Air Station close out the line, with the Riquezas Keys off to the northwest. That is the end of Leonida.

**The north edge.** Up along US 82 the map hands over to West Vice-Dale County. Breakwater Quarry and the Sahara Network Data Center are on that side of the line.

Mariana is where the state stops pretending it can be paved. **Know the water, or stay on the one road.**
