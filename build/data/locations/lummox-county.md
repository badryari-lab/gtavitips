Head north out of [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) long enough and the flat land gives up. Lummox County covers the whole top of Leonida, Gulf side to Atlantic side, and it holds the only real high ground in the state. [Mount Kalaga National Park](/gameplay/locations/mount-kalaga-national-park) fills the middle of the county, with [Mount Kalaga](/gameplay/locations/mount-kalaga) rising out of it.

**This is hill country, and it looks more like north Georgia than South Florida.** Forest, ridges, rivers running down to the bays, small towns with a lot of road between them. Nobody up here is pretending to be Vice City, and nobody up here wants to.

The coast is all bays and islands. Okchiloha Bay and San Malo Bay cut into the northwest, Partridge Bay sits in the northeast corner, and Millport Basin closes off the east. Island groups trail off both coasts.

**The roads are few and long.** US 628 loops across the north of the county, from Grayson on the west side, over the hills past Moreau and on east toward Millport. I-297 runs along the southern edge, roughly the line where Lummox drops down into [Kelly County](/gameplay/locations/kelly-county) and [Leonard County](/gameplay/locations/leonard-county). I-97 comes up the Atlantic coast as far as Millport. Miss a turn up here and the next one is a while away.
%%%GROUND%%%
**Bonanza and the north coast.** [Bonanza](/gameplay/locations/bonanza) is the northern tip of the mainland, on Okchiloha Bay. Just south of it, Moreau sits on the Moreau River, with the MacAlister Gold Mine on the hills nearby. The Saint Attaway Islands lie off the coast to the east, with Hyde Island further out.

**The northeast.** Partridge Bay and the Frampton Islands mark the corner of the state. Inland, East Lummox County holds the Tallaloko Lakeside Resort on Lake Tallaloko, a short way off US 628. It is the closest thing the county has to a getaway.

**Millport.** [Millport](/gameplay/locations/millport) sits on Millport Basin at the east end of the county, right where Lummox runs into North Leonard County. It is the top of I-97 and the quick way back south.

**The west side.** Grayson is the town on San Malo Bay, on US 628. South of it, South West Lummox County is the hill ground that rolls down toward I-297 and the Kelly County farms. The Featherstone Islands sit off the coast to the north of Grayson, and the Zephyr Islands lie further down the Gulf side, above Draper Island.

**The park.** [Mount Kalaga National Park](/gameplay/locations/mount-kalaga-national-park) takes up the center of the county between Grayson and Millport. It is the wild part of a wild county: trails, wildlife, steep ground that punishes the wrong car, and the kind of people who moved up here specifically so nobody would come looking.

**Bring fuel and bring patience.** Lummox is big, quiet and a long drive from anywhere that matters to Vice City. That is the whole appeal.
