Millport is in the northeast corner of the state, on Millport Basin, right where Lummox County meets North Leonard County. Little Hardin Island sits offshore. [Lake Leonida](/gameplay/locations/lake-leonida) is south and west.

**I-97 runs north past town on its way up the coast.** Route 628 heads west toward Talaloko Lakeside Resort and the rest of Lummox. South of town the map marks the Mount Hailes Country Club and Richard Barebone State Park.

That is the map's whole account of it. Quiet coast, a long way from [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city).
%%%GROUND%%%
I-97 is the fast line back south toward [Waning Sands](/gameplay/locations/waning-sands) and the city. **Millport is a drive, not a detour.**
