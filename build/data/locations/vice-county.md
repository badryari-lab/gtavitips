Vice County is the jurisdiction that makes Vice City function. The hotels need staff. The clubs need supply chains. The tourists need airports, roads, and everything that happens before they arrive and after they leave. All of that infrastructure lives in the county rather than the city, and the county is where most of the state's actual population lives and works.

The territory covers a significant range. The eastern edge of the county runs directly into the urban grid of [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city). Move west and the density drops through residential suburbs into the commercial and industrial corridors that service the port and the logistics network. Continue further and the land opens into agricultural flat, wetland margins, and the road infrastructure connecting Leonida's population centres.

**The county is not the postcard version of Leonida.** The county is what the postcard version runs on. Distribution centres, fuel depots, correctional facilities, law enforcement staging areas, the road network that trucks use at three in the morning. This is the functional layer of the state.
%%%GROUND%%%
Vice County rewards local knowledge over speed. The rural road network has fewer witnesses and slower response times than the urban core, but the open geography removes the cover that the city provides. A car visible from half a kilometre away is a different problem from a car that can turn into a parking structure.

**The suburb fringe is the most operationally useful zone in the county.** Dense enough for cover, sparse enough for movement, and close enough to the city to use [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) infrastructure on short notice. [State Police](/gameplay/gangs-and-factions/leonida-state-police) patrol the highway corridors but the residential grid beneath them is lighter coverage.

The industrial zones between the suburb fringe and the port corridor are useful at night and exposed during business hours. Warehouses, logistics compounds, and light industrial units line the access roads. The fencing is present but not continuous.
