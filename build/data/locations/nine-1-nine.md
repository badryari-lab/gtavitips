Nine 1 Nine is a [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) nightlife venue with a name that plays two ways: the emergency number, and the hour a club actually gets going. Whether that's intentional, nobody's saying. The venue's real either way.

**Vice City nightlife runs on tiers.** The [Malibu Club](/gameplay/locations/malibu-club) is the legacy name. Bahama Mamas plays to the tourists. Where Nine 1 Nine sits between them, nobody's spelled out yet.

Vice City clubs work the way they always have here: mission location, money mover, and the one room loud enough and locked-down enough for every power player in the city to overlap without making a scene.

The club environment creates the specific operational conditions that [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) nightlife has always provided. High ambient noise, controlled entry, and a clientele that is not inclined to cooperate with law enforcement on anything that happens inside.

**Nine 1 Nine's exact position in the Vice City nightlife district determines which entrance and exit routes apply.** The district grid is consistent with [South Beach](/gameplay/locations/south-beach) and the adjacent entertainment corridor.
