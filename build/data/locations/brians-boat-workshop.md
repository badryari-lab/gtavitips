Brian's Boat Workshop is [Brian Heder](/gameplay/characters/brian-heder)'s boat yard in the [Leonida Keys](/gameplay/locations/leonida-keys). The business is boat repair. **The boats that come through are a mix of fishing boats, weekend cruisers, and the kind that wants work done away from the main channels with a minimum of questions.**

Brian has run product through the Keys since the golden age of smuggling, and the yard is where it still moves. So do the rent money, the favors and whatever his people bring back. He sets it up. Somebody else does the lifting.

His third wife, [Lori](/gameplay/characters/lori-heder), is in the business with him. [Jason Duval](/gameplay/characters/jason-duval) and [Cal Hampton](/gameplay/characters/cal-hampton) are two of Brian's regulars, and the yard is never far from whatever they are working on.
%%%GROUND%%%
The water is what makes this place useful. Boats come and go straight off the yard, no road required. Whatever leaves by water never shows up on anything a road patrol would see.

**Talk to Brian, use the water, let the work order do the talking for you.** That's the whole arrangement.

There is one road down through the Keys if you want the normal approach. Everything else around the yard is open water.
