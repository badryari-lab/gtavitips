Route 1A leaves the mainland past [Hamlet](/gameplay/locations/hamlet), skips over the Shoeless Keys and lands on Key Lento. It is the first big island of the [Leonida Keys](/gameplay/locations/leonida-keys), and the one most people mean when they say they are going down to the Keys.

**One highway, shops, bars and houses on either side, water at both ends.** The Rusty Anchor bar and the Wonder Whale shop, with its big whale out front, are local landmarks. [Brian's Boat Works & Marina](/gameplay/locations/brians-boat-works-and-marina) is here too.

[Jason](/gameplay/characters/jason-duval) knows these roads. This is his part of Leonida.

The real-world model is Key Largo, the top of the Florida Keys.
%%%GROUND%%%
The water is the point. [Kenton Rees](/gameplay/characters/kenton-rees) runs his treasure dives out of Key Lento, and the reefs around the island are good ground for [diving](/gameplay/activities/diving).

**Route 1A is the only road.** South, it runs on past Maroon Key and Abismo Key to Southwind and the Mile Zero Air Station. North, it goes back to the mainland. Block it and a boat is the way out.
