Shore Court is the drop point for the ['55 Vapid Stanier](/gameplay/vehicles/vapid-stanier) pre-order bonus. The property comes with a personal garage, a weapon locker, and stolen goods storage already installed. You don't build it out. You show up and it's ready.

The location sits on the [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) coast. **Coastal positioning in Leonida means water access as well as road access.**  An exit option that the rest of the city doesn't always offer. What else sits nearby is not confirmed before launch.

The weapon locker and stolen goods storage aren't decorative. They work from day one. Shore Court is not just a place to keep the Stanier. It's a functional base.
%%%GROUND%%%
A pre-installed garage, locker, and storage unit means Shore Court is operational the moment you arrive. No scouting a build-out, no waiting on upgrades. **Whatever runs through the Stanier or sits in that locker is ready from day one.**

Coastal and on the Vice City edge, the property has the escape options of any waterfront address: road out through the city grid, or water out through whatever's moored nearby. Nobody's confirmed what's actually next door, so treat the immediate surroundings as unknown until launch.
