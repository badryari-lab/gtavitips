Little Cuba sits right in the middle of [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city). The glass of [Downtown](/gameplay/locations/downtown-vice-city) starts just east of it. Salton is on the west side, Rockridge to the north, and the runways of [VCIA](/gameplay/locations/vice-city-international-airport) are not far past Salton.

**This is Leonida's Little Havana.** Cuban families, Cuban businesses, Cuban roots. It is a working neighbourhood, low-rise and busy, a long way in spirit from the hotel strip across the bay.

The name has history. The old Vice City had its Little Havana. This one took the name of the island itself.
%%%GROUND%%%
Little Cuba is close to everything. Downtown is a short drive east, the interstate runs past the city core, and the airport sits to the southwest. **Central means busy streets and cops who are never far off.**

Northwest, past Balsero, the map marks the Tisha-Wocka Flea Market.
