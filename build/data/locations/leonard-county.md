Drive north out of [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) on I-97 and the city doesn't stop. It just spreads out, slows down and gets a lawn. That is Leonard County: the long stretch between the city and the hills, with [Lake Leonida](/gameplay/locations/lake-leonida) in one corner and the Atlantic down the other side.

**The lake is the county's center of gravity.** The biggest body of water in the state fills the northwest of Leonard, with the Mount Hailes Country Club on its northeast shore. [Ambrosia](/gameplay/locations/ambrosia) watches it from the other side of the line in [Kelly County](/gameplay/locations/kelly-county).

The rest is flat lowland running down to the coast. Out east, the barrier strip holds Vinewood Beach, and Little Hardin Island sits offshore further north. South Leonard County is the open ground below Belville, and past that the map hands over to [Vice-Dale County](/gameplay/locations/vice-county). Leaf Links and the Decor District are already city territory.

**I-97 does all the heavy lifting.** It runs up the coast from Vice City through Waning Sands and on to Millport. CR 62 crosses the middle of the county east to west through Belville and carries on into Kelly. US 35 runs along the west edge past Ambrosia, and I-297 crosses above the lake on its way across the north.
%%%GROUND%%%
**Waning Sands.** [Waning Sands](/gameplay/locations/waning-sands) is the coast town, right on I-97. Sabal Palms is its north end, and the Evergreen Mall of Waning Sands is the big stop on the highway. Vinewood Beach is just across the water on the barrier strip. If you want sand without the Vice Beach crowd, this is the drive.

**The lake.** [Lake Leonida](/gameplay/locations/lake-leonida) is open water as far as you can see, with largemouth bass in it and room for a boat to disappear for an afternoon. [Fishing](/gameplay/activities/fishing) is the obvious play. The Mount Hailes Country Club sits on the northeast shore, and Richard Barebone State Park lies east of it, out by I-97 near the coast.

**Belville.** Belville is the inland town, in the middle of the county on CR 62. Chateau Cellerica sits just north of town and Cellerica just south. Below it, South Leonard County runs on toward the edge of Vice City.

**North Leonard County.** The top of the county is quieter still. [Millport](/gameplay/locations/millport) marks the corner where Leonard runs into [Lummox County](/gameplay/locations/lummox-county), and Little Hardin Island sits off the coast nearby.

Leonard is where Vice City goes when it wants a yard, a mall and a lake within reach. **Close enough to the action to drive in, far enough out to pretend it isn't there.**
