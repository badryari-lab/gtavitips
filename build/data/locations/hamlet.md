Head south out of [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) long enough and the towers turn into Hamlet. It is the bottom end of [Vice-Dale County](/gameplay/locations/vice-county): [Grassrivers](/gameplay/locations/grassrivers) to the west, South Vice-Dale to the east, and Route 1A heading southeast for [Key Lento](/gameplay/locations/key-lento).

Two names on the map do the talking. **Hamlet Correctional Institution on the north side of town, Boone Speedway on the south.** Between them is a flat, low town that most people pass through on the way somewhere else.

The real-world model is Homestead, the last city before the Florida Keys.
%%%GROUND%%%
US 35 runs down the east side of town from the city. Route 1A starts southeast of Hamlet and is the road to the Keys. **If you are driving to the islands, you are going through here.**

West of town the wetlands begin, and the grid gives way to swamp quickly.
