Bonanza sits on the northern tip of the state, in Lummox County. Okchiloha Bay wraps the west side. The Moreau River runs to the east, with the Saint Attaway Islands offshore and the Featherstone Islands out west.

**South of town the map marks the MacAlister Gold Mine.** Moreau is a short drive southeast, and Route 628 carries on from there into the hills around [Mount Kalaga National Park](/gameplay/locations/mount-kalaga-national-park).

That is the whole picture. A town at the end of the road, about as far from [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) as you can drive.
%%%GROUND%%%
Getting here takes a while. The interstates stay further south. **Route 628 is the road that gets you near.** Bonanza rewards the people who bother.
