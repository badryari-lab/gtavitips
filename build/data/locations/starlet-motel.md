The Starlet Motel is the kind of accommodation that Leonida has along every major road corridor that runs far enough from [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) to need a place to stop. Single-storey, car-facing rooms, a vending machine in the breezeway, and a sign with a letter missing that has been missing for long enough that the front desk has stopped noticing.

Leonida's always had this category of accommodation. The motel people use when they need somewhere to be that's not on anyone's radar. The Starlet fits that role here. **It is not the [Ocean View](/gameplay/locations/ocean-view-hotel). That is exactly the point.**

The roadside position means vehicle access is the primary function. You pull in, you park directly outside the room, and the road you came from is still visible from the window. The Starlet Motel is a location defined by proximity to the road rather than by what surrounds it on the other three sides.
%%%GROUND%%%
The Starlet Motel operates outside the tourist corridor intensity. Roadside coverage is standard county patrol rather than the tourist-zone density of [South Beach](/gameplay/locations/south-beach). The motel itself is not a notable location to patrol around unless it becomes notable.

**Vehicle access and exit is the primary practical feature.** Direct road access, parking directly adjacent to the room, and the ability to leave quickly if the road ahead is clear. The motel does not generate reasons to linger.

The clientele at a Leonida roadside motel at two in the morning is a specific demographic that has chosen this specific type of accommodation for reasons that vary but share a common characteristic: nobody books the Starlet because they want to.
