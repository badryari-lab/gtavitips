The Leonida Keys are the southern extension of the state. The islands run in a chain from the lower [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) coastline out into the open Atlantic. The further out you go, the less the mainland applies. The road ends. The water begins. Everything that happens between those two points is a boat ride from any consequence.

The Keys have been a fixture of Florida geography, and Leonida's version of it, since Vice City established the template. **Coral reefs, narrow channel navigation, and the specific social ecosystem that develops when the nearest police station is a long boat ride away.** Fishing operations, tourist charters, dive boats, and the businesses that exist because the Keys are the correct distance from Vice City for certain arrangements.

The shallow water systems between the islands create a navigation environment that rewards local knowledge. The main channels are marked. The secondary routes are not. A boat that knows which unmarked route connects which island pair is moving through a geography that pursuit cannot follow without the same knowledge.

**This is [Jason Duval's](/gameplay/characters/jason-duval) home.** Not a hideout, not a temporary arrangement — the Keys are named directly as where he's from, the place he actually knows rather than the place he operates. It shows in how he reads the water and the roads down there, a level of local knowledge that doesn't come from a job, only from having grown up with it.
%%%GROUND%%%
Watercraft is the primary transport requirement in the Keys. The main island chain has road access. The outer positions do not. A vehicle is useful getting to the Keys. In the Keys, the boat is the vehicle.

**The reef systems create a pursuit deterrent that has nothing to do with law enforcement.** Fast pursuit craft drawing more water than the channel depth cannot follow a shallow-draft boat through the correct passage. This geographic fact has been commercially useful to Keys operators for several decades in the real world and will translate directly to Leonida.

Coast Guard coverage is the primary law enforcement presence in the outer Keys. Response to the inner island chain is faster but still measured in minutes rather than seconds. The isolation that makes the Keys attractive to tourists makes them operationally interesting for different reasons.
