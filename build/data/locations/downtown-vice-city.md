Downtown is the bit of [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) you see from the water. It sits on the bay, with [Little Cuba](/gameplay/locations/little-cuba) on its west side, Crosstown and FLAM to the north and Tequesta to the south.

**I-397 cuts along its southern edge and heads for the bay.** Across the water lie Waxman Island and then [Vice Beach](/gameplay/locations/vice-beach).

The towers say money. What goes on behind the glass is another matter.
%%%GROUND%%%
Downtown is central to almost every drive in the city. The interstates meet nearby, the bay is on the doorstep and the beach is one crossing away. **Expect traffic, expect cops, and expect to be seen.**
