Capri is on the other coast. While Vice City faces the Atlantic, Capri looks out over Capri Sound on the Gulf side of Leonida. Tortoise Lake sits inside town, Belpoint lies across the water to the southwest, and the Nehrling River runs southeast.

**US 183 runs straight through it.** I-404 swings past on the east side, which puts [Port Gellhorn](/gameplay/locations/port-gellhorn) one drive north and the city one drive east.

The real-world model is Naples, Florida. The name is a nod to it: Naples and Capri sit side by side in Italy.
%%%GROUND%%%
Capri is far from the Vice City crowds. **The interstate is the quick way in and out.** The Sound is the slow one.

[Grassrivers](/gameplay/locations/grassrivers) and the wetlands of Mariana County start to the southeast.
