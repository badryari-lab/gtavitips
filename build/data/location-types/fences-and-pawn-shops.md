A robbed safe pays in things, not cash. **Narcotics, jewelry and Marked Bills only turn into money at a fence**, and Leonida has seven of them. Each one rates each kind of goods from Very Low to High, so where you sell matters as much as what you stole.

Drive the stash to the fence yourself, sell it through the partner menu for a little less, or, from Chapter 3, send it from a garage for a fee. Early on the money arrives by mailbox, later straight into the bank. Marked Bills are their own problem: no fence touches them until you own the Dye Pack Jammer, which arrives in Chapter 3.

Two fences only deal in cars. [Moses](/gameplay/characters/moses-watkins) in Port Gellhorn takes anything without a tracker, so scan it with the WAiNK app first. Randy in Balsero takes the expensive ones with advanced trackers. **Pawn shops are the other side of the deal**: from Chapter 2 they are Smash 'n' Grab targets, listed under Robberies on the map, and what you take from the cases ends up at a fence.
