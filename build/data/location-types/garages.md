Leonida is full of cars worth keeping and nowhere to keep them. **Every garage space you own lets you register one more vehicle in your name.** No space, no keeper.

Eight garages are for sale on the **Vice City Real Estate** app, which lands on your phone in Chapter 2. They run from $4,000 for Mitchell Auto Center in Port Gellhorn to $40,000 for Atlantic Court in Ocean Beach, and every one of them holds a single car. Pay for the address, not the room. Buy all eight and the Little Boxes trophy pops.

Four more come free as the story moves: one in Port Gellhorn in Chapter 2, one in Ocean Beach in Chapter 4 and two in Vice City in Chapter 5, with room for up to three cars. Paradise Garage in Watson Bay comes with the Ultimate Edition, and the Vintage Vice City Pack adds one at [Shore Court](/gameplay/locations/shore-court). Neither counts toward the eight.
