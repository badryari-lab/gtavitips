Every crime has a bill, and **the police collect when they catch you.** Get busted and the cash in your pocket is gone, along with any loose or temporary weapons. A small fee comes off too. You wake up at the nearest safe spot, not in a cell. Money in the bank or in a stash stays yours, so bank it before the job.

**Your car is the other cost.** Get busted or die in your personal vehicle, or leave it inside a police search area after a crime, and it goes to the impound. Stolen cars never do. Getting it back means paying a fee in the Scooter Bros app, free once a day with the premium tier, or walking into the impound lot and driving it out before anyone notices. Do it quickly if there were stash bags in the trunk, because they can go missing or end up in a police car nearby.

Losing the police is about information. Break their line of sight, leave the search area and let the icons that show what they know clear. They remember the car and your look, so swap both.
