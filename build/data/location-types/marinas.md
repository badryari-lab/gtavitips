Leonida runs on water as much as on asphalt, but **nobody sells you a boat.** No marina has a shop, a rental desk or a slip to buy. The marinas are where the boats sit, and taking one works the way it does on land: small boats hotwire from Chapter 1, the keyless speedboats and yachts need the Key Cloner.

Don't get attached. A boat you took and tied up at a dock stays around for a while, then clears once the story moves on, and boats can't go into a garage the way cars do. Ultimate Edition owners have one that stays: the [Shitzu Squalo](/gameplay/vehicles/shitzu-squalo), moored at Washington Beach.

The two Key Lento marinas sit side by side, the Marina Club on the shore and [Brian's Boat Works](/gameplay/locations/brians-boat-works-and-marina) down the channel behind it. And the rod works from any dock: walk out, pick it from the Equipment tab and cast.
