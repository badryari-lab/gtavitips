You don't pick where you live. **The story hands you a place to crash, one per chapter**, and none of them are for sale: Key Lento first, then a room at the Starlet Motel, an apartment in Bayside and a suite in Ocean Beach. If you want property with your name on it, that's the garages.

What a safehouse gives you is downtime. Sleep in the bed and the night skips, with full health and stamina when you get up. The wardrobe changes your clothes, your cars park outside, and a quick trim or shave works at home, though a new hairstyle means a trip to a salon. Jason's place on Key Lento hides some cash under the bedroom floor.

**Saving doesn't need any of this.** Open the pause menu and pick Save Game whenever you are in free roam and off a mission. Only the prologue and missions themselves lock it.
