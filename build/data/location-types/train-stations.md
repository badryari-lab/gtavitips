---
The train doesn't care about your wanted level. It runs fixed routes, from the towers of Downtown Vice City out to Port Gellhorn on the Gulf side and up to Millport and Grayson in Lummox.

Walk into a station and wait. When the train pulls in, step aboard and pick your stop. The city has its own cluster of stops, Downtown, Courthouse, the Arts Center and Crosstown, a few blocks apart. Out in the counties the stations sit right in the middle of town.

Stations show as a train icon once the fog clears. **Map > Vehicles > Train Stations** lists every one you have found, and holding down on the D-pad pulls up the nearest stops.
