Leonida is big and your car is hot. **The bus is the quiet way across it.** Wait at a stop, climb on, take a seat and pick where you are going. Ride it out and watch the city go by, or hold **Triangle** or **Y** and skip straight there. The fare rises with distance and stays well under a taxi. With GTA+ it costs nothing.

The **ferry** links Waxman Island and Dalton Island. Walk up to the terminal, pay $50 and you are across. Nobody hands you the wheel.

Nobody sells you a ticket. **The airports and airfields are where the planes are**, and you fly them yourself. Vice City International keeps its airside under guard, and lifting a jet there puts the police on you. The small strips, like the one at Port Gellhorn, look the other way.
