Hair grows back, so go wild. **Every barber and salon in Leonida changes how Jason or Lucia looks, and some of them have styles nobody else does.** Jason sits down for a cut, a new colour or a different beard. Lucia gets hairstyles and colour too, and the beauty salons add makeup and nails.

The standard styles are on offer everywhere. Signature cuts are not: a handful only show up at one particular salon, so a look you saw somewhere else might need a drive. Owners of the Ultimate Edition get [Sara's Unisex Salon](/gameplay/brands/saras-unisex-salon) on State Route 61 in Hamlet, and the pre-order Vice City Style looks are picked up in the salon chair.

Salons show a scissors icon once the fog clears. **Map > Style > Hair & Beauty** lists them with a counter of the ones you have found, and a lip icon marks the beauty salons. Most of them sit in Vice-Dale County, with a few out in the Keys, Kelly and Leonard.
