---
What you wear is part of the job. The police hunt for a description, and a new shirt is the cheapest way to stop matching it. Vice City has most of the racks: Downtown, Ocean Beach and the Decor District are lined with them, and the Keys run on gift shops.

Walk up to a rack and the menu opens: price, whether you already own the piece, and a preview on whoever you are playing. **Buy** sends it to the wardrobe, **Buy and Wear** puts it on right there. Jason and Lucia keep separate wardrobes at the safehouse, and most stores have a changing room for clothes you already own.

Stores show as an orange T-shirt once the fog clears, shoe shops as an orange shoe. **Map > Style** sorts them into Casual & Street, Sportswear, Formal, Souvenir & Swimwear, Thrift and Footwear. Three Ultimate Edition extras sit on top: [Stock 305](/gameplay/brands/stock-305) in Stockyard, [Goodtime Gear](/gameplay/brands/goodtime-gear) at The Wonder Whale, and [Vice City Style](/gameplay/brands/vice-city-style), which skips the store and lands in your wardrobe in Chapter 4.
