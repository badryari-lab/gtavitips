Every block in Leonida has a mart, and most forecourts have one bolted on. **Grab a snack, top up, keep moving.** Fast food gives a short boost to health, stamina or focus, some of it pushes you past your normal maximum for a while, and you can eat at the wheel. Convenience stores also stock Zombix, a heal you carry in your pocket, plus groceries for the fridge at home.

The fast food chains are their own crowd: Pollo Diablo, Marbles, Taco Bomb, Peewees and Chacey's, one [Burger Shot](/gameplay/brands/burgershot) on Ocean Beach and two waffle houses, [Hank's Waffles](/gameplay/brands/hanks-waffles) and Frank's.

**Every counter is also a till.** Pull a weapon and the clerk empties the register. Bigger stores keep a safe in the back: get the four-digit code out of an employee or find where someone wrote it down, or blow it and live with the noise. The register pays pocket money. The safe holds the real cash and the jewelry a fence wants. Convenience stores show as a shopping bag and fast food as a burger once the fog clears, both under **Map > Food & Drink**.
