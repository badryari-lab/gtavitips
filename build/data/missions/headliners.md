As Lucia, take the DJ booth during the sound check and set up the light show: move the rigs, pick colours, test smoke, pyro and lasers.

As Jason, work the door. Assign VIP tables from each guest's cash offer and follower count, look after the special guests, free up a booth when one is needed and keep order once the show starts.
