Drive to the handover at the cement factory with Andrés. As Lucia, slip away, climb up for a view and use the binoculars to find where the boy is held, then sneak in with a silenced pistol.

When it turns loud, swap between Jason protecting Andrés out front and Lucia getting the boy down through the factory. Finish with a chase by car and bike, shooting from the back seat.
