Drive out to the Thrillbilly Mud Club and park at the entrance. Buy something at the merch stall, because your clothes are about to get filthy, then ride down to the track with Wyman.

Rent a bike at Rooster's Rentals, check in with the race organizer and run the race. Finish ahead of one of Billy's riders and talk to Billy at the end.
