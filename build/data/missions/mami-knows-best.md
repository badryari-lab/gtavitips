Meet Mia in Peacock Bay and drive her to the marked house in Hamlet. Follow her through the rooms, help her up at the entrance, then clear the front lawn into the dumpster.

Out back, pull the mattress out of the pool with her and be ready when the water moves. When the time comes, climb into the excavator across the street and wreck everything in reach, then drive Mia to her office.
