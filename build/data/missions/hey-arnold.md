You meet Brian at a hotel pool bar, take the wheel of his truck and pick Cal up at a liquor store. Cal tracks a phone south down the causeway to an old radar station.

After the talk at the station, a car takes off with men on motorbikes riding cover. Chase it along the coast and across a beach, shooting the bikes off the road, then run the driver down on foot. At the end you choose what happens next.
