Meet Dre'Quan at the Jack of Hearts and sit in on the studio session, then go up to Boobie's office for the job. Ride with Dre'Quan to Imperial Plaza in La Perle and climb to the room upstairs.

In the kitchen, look at both packages and take the cut or the pure product. Drive across Crosstown to the buyers, hand over the bag, collect the backpack of cash and drop Dre'Quan in Southside to finish.
