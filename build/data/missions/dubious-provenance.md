Grab the bag from the closet of the Bayside apartment and drive west to Capri. The street door is locked, so take the outside stairs and show the statue upstairs. On your way out, decide whether to leave it with the man who offers to appraise it.

The two optional follow-ups start later: a quiet break-in at the store and a search next door, then a chase over water and land. Finish both for the payout at the end.
