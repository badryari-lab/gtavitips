Drive to the restaurant on the east side of the city and take the lift to the roof. Hand out drinks, order at the table, then join Brian at the railing until the game switches you to Lucia.

As Lucia, take the white van across the bridge and drive into the dockyard. Find the yellow SCOOP container on top of the middle stack and cut the lock. When the truck pulls away, grab a bike, chase it, jump onto the trailer, reverse the car out, lose the police and drive it into the lockup in the north-west of the city.
