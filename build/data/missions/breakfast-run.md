Head back to the motel when Jason calls, then drive to Hank's Waffles. Aim at the staff, take the cash and get out before the timer runs down.

The spree rolls on from there: a liquor store, a thrift store and a few more, switching between Jason and Lucia. Finish in the car, lose the police and drive home.
