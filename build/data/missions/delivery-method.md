Pick up the flash drive at Andrea's shop, take the delivery bike and ride to the TSS office. Talk your way past the gate guard and try the reception computer.

Pull the fire alarm, run upstairs to the security department and plug in at a desk with database access before the fire crews arrive. Then pull the drive and ride away clean.
