Pick up Andrés on Starfish Island and drive him to The Vault. Inside, you run his errands: fetch a drink, take a photo, bring the right people to his booth, talk to a rival table and fetch something from a car in the alley.

When the night turns, fight your way out of the club, ride shotgun with a machine gun through the car chase, then get him to a rooftop helipad and hold off the helicopters coming after you.
