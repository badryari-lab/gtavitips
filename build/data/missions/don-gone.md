You drive to the trailer on Key Lento, break open the door and pack the bag with everything on the list. Outside, handle the three men who pull up in a jeep, then stow the bag in the car's trunk.

Shake any police, drive off Key Lento and find a quiet spot. Take the revolver from the trunk, then burn the car or sink it and walk away.
