Meet Dunk at his trailer and follow him to the store. Wait out of sight, then go in after the doctor and drive his car to the docks.

When it turns into a fight, escape the yard and lose the chase. Drive to the meeting spot, survive the shootout, find Dunk and drive him home. Then bring the pills to Jason at the motel.
