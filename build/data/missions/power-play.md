Start as Lucia at the crew's base, then switch to Jason. Get inside unseen by the roof, open the locked planning room and photograph what the crew is hiding.

Back as Lucia, block the road with cones, grab a rifle and cover the armored truck job as the police close in. Fight through a construction site onto the highway, steal a car and lose the heat.
