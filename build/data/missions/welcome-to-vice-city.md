Follow the GPS off the highway into Crosstown and park outside the Jack of Hearts. Skip the queue, walk round to the back door and follow Dre'Quan inside.

Spend the play money on the dancers if you like, then go back to Dre'Quan and head upstairs. When the scene ends, drive to the Bayside apartment, park in the covered spot and walk up to the second floor.
