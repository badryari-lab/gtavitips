Meet Dre'Quan in Tequesta and ride up to the roof. As Lucia, raise the phone camera and shoot the Dimez as they pose in the helicopter while Jason holds it steady.

When the job goes sideways, chase the car in the helicopter as Jason, then switch to Lucia on a bike when it drives into a building. Fight through the junkyard and the alley under the highway, and reach the car at the end.
