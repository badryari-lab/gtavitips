You talk to Lucia at the house, stop at the liquor store for a six-pack and find Cal out back on his dock. Take his boat out to the usual spot and catch a fish.

Head back, pull up at the dock, open the grill and cook the catch. When Lucia wanders off, follow her out front and drive her home.
