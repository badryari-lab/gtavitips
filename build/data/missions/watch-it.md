Meet Dre'Quan at the shop and wait while he argues with the clerk. Drive him to the parking garage, head up to the top floor and back him up when the talk turns physical.

Deal with the gunmen who roll up by car and from the stairs, then get out before the police arrive. Pick an outfit at the clothes store, drive to the hotel and follow Dre'Quan through to the meeting.
