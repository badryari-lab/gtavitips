Start at the gym, take Kordell's car and drive him to a warehouse on Northwest 8th Avenue. Go in alone, ask around for his contact and win the cage fight that follows.

Then drive him to Riverbank in Little Cuba, park nearby, follow him inside and keep quiet at the table.
