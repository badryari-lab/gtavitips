Talk to the concierge in the Y Hotel lobby and take the hotel car service to Status Auto Imports. Find the salesman, follow him across the showroom and look over the stat cards.

Choose a car for Jason, then one for Lucia. Once she takes off, chase her down and race her back to the Y Hotel.
