Pick the job from your optional list and meet Billy in Port Gellhorn. Get on the dirt bike he gives you and follow him across the fields to the ambush spot.

Chase the armored van with the crew until it crashes. Plant the explosive on the back, handle the guard, grab the bag and follow Billy off road until the police give up.
