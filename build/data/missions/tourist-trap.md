You start as Jason, meeting Lucia on her work-release shift, then put on the disguises and walk into the Grassrivers Visitor Center. Find locker 17 on the second floor and take the bag.

The second half is all Lucia: stay close to Jason, fight across the grounds pillar to pillar and get him into the boat at the marina. Lose four stars on open water, then steer for Cal's house.
