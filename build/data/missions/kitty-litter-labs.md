Go to a purple search area on the map, find the building leaking pink smoke and get inside. Deal with whoever is cooking, then wreck the equipment with fire or explosives.

Tyrone sends the first location as soon as you finish his last job, and he or Dook give out more when you ask. You can also hit any area without waiting for a tip.
