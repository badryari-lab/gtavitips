Drive to the airport, check in at the gate and go to the office to get hired. Ride along with your new boss, hook the baggage tug to two carts and drive out to the plane.

Throw the bags up to him, then make an excuse and head for the security office. Slip into the restroom, plant the device behind the toilet, and go back to tell him you want the job.
