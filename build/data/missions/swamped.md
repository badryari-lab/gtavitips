You meet Brian at the boat yard and ride in his pickup to the Kingfishers camp, a long way north off the Keys. Walk into the yard with him and inspect the pen when prompted.

Fight your way across the camp to the water, take off in the helicopter and cover the stilt village from the air. Destroy the truck, then finish the chase on an airboat and jump across to the other boat.
