You start in a cell. Talk to the women around you, then follow the guard through the common room to the counselor's office. That conversation drops you into a flashback on the outside.

In the flashback you hold up a store counter: aim at the teller, threaten, take the cash. Shake off a customer who grabs you, run for the rooftops, jack a car and try to lose two stars of heat. Then head back the way the marker points.
