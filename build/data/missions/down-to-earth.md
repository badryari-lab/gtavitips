You wait until morning, see Brian in his office and scrub a hull clean with the pressure washer. Then walk to the end of the dock, take the seaplane up and land beside a fishing boat out in the bay.

Survive the standoff on the water, then fly to each drop and set down beside it. For the last one stay airborne and pace a boat low over the river. Fly home, land by the dock and hide the bag in the shed under the house.
