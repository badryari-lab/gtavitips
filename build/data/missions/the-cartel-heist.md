Meet the crew at the planning room, drive downtown and blow the cell towers from your phone. Walk into the bank and wait for the signal, then take the lobby as Jason while Lucia heads for the vault floor.

Clear the roof, drill and crack the vault, and bag the cash. Hold off the police, get out through the basement, steal a SWAT truck and defend the bus until Jason can jump aboard.
