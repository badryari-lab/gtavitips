Read the note on the door of Mia's office in Hamlet, then drive to the house and search it. Take Mia's car and tail the other one from a distance until it turns into a blocked alley, then go in on foot.

After the talking, drive carefully to each property on the map and follow Mia through the last house. Clear the backyard and the front, then get back inside fast.
