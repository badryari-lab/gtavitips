Sign the papers at Abe's office, head to the penthouse, make Lucia's pool shot and take the drive to the drop as Jason.

The second half is one long gunfight in Hamlet: barricade a door with lockers, push through to the armory, clear a path to the warehouse across the street and hold out against a heavy assault.
