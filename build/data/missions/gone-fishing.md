You take the wheel of Cal's boat and chase a low-flying plane across the shallows, slowing beside each package so Cal can hook it out of the water. Then you scope a dock through binoculars and go ashore on your own.

Creep through a swamp camp, win a fistfight, heal up and fight your way to a seaplane. Take off from the water, fly back to Brian's marina, set it down and taxi in. Drop Cal at his place and head home to Key Lento.
