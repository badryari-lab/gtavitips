Steal a car, shake any cops and drive it into the open bay at Watkins Auto Parts. Follow Moses into the yard and look over the cars he points at.

Use the Slim Jim on the alarmed one without setting it off, walk back to the shop, then respray and register the car you came in with. It is yours from then on.
