Take Jason and Lucia to the garage on Waxman Island, punch in the code and get in the car. As Lucia, drive to the pickup on the south side of Ocean Beach.

When the police pile on, switch to Jason and fight them off, then go back to Lucia, jump the bridge to the Rialto Islands, swap into the van and drive to the drop in Bayside.
