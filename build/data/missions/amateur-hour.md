Talk to Cisco on the Trauma Floor at Ajax Gym, change at your locker and follow him out the back to his car. Drive to the pick-up spot, park in the marked space and wait on the bench.

When the camper shows and bolts, get back in and chase it down. Wait while Cisco collects, then drive him back behind the gym and park in his spot.
