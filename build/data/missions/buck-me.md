You take Lucia's call, drive the truck to the Fleeca branch off the highway and ask the teller about an account. Follow the manager back to his glass office and sit through the pitch.

Back home, open BuckMe on your phone and pick your checking account. Then pull cash from the stash under the floorboards and feed it into the nearest ATM.
