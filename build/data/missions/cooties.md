A couple of days after Ride or Die, Dre'Quan calls. Head to the shoot in Crosstown and drive the car in circles for the camera, then through the streets.

When it stops, deal with the two men, take some swings at the car with a bat and tag it with the spray can. Shake the crew that comes after you and get the car to the studio.
