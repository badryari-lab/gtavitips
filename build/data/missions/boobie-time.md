Meet Boobie at the Jack of Hearts and take the driver's seat. Make both pickups, then drive to the club by the river in the middle of the city.

When the bag goes missing, chase the thieves down the river on a jet ski and grab it. Ride back, get Boobie in the car and drive to the smoke shop across the river to finish.
