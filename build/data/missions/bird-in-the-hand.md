Fly Vinny's helicopter above the coast guard base and scout it through binoculars as Lucia, then drop into the sea and ride a boat to the military base.

Go in through the inlet tunnel, reach the hangar by the radio tower through the roof hatch and open the doors. Hold the hangar while the cargo helicopter gets ready, then shoot down the jets on the way out.
