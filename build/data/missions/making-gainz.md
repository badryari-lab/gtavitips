Head to Ajax Gym as Lucia. Find Nate upstairs by the benches and keep at him until he gives you the time of day.

Hit the bench press with him spotting, follow him to the squat rack and outside for deadlifts, then take the free bike across the pool. Play through the training montage and its choices. When you leave, link up with Jason north of the gym and drive to the planning room.
