Go back to the motel room and use the bathroom door to start. As Jason, pick up the shotgun and fight your way across the lot to the cafeteria.

As Lucia, hold out inside, then follow Jason to the road. Take the bike, chase his car, jump across when the prompt shows, and shoot the police off your tail until the cutscene.
