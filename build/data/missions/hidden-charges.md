---
Answer the call to the planning room, get in the van loaded with explosives and drive it into the marked garage downtown.

Take the bag up to each rooftop cell tower in turn, switching between Jason and Lucia if you like, and plant the charges. Then jump with a parachute and get out of the police search area.
