You meet Joey under the canopy at Coastal Auto Care, fetch the Ganado and roll it into his shop. Inside, accept the free repair, then go to Performance and restore the stock engine.

Next, Bodywork and Respray: any color in the recommended row is free. Pick one, drive out, and listen to Joey's offer on the way.
