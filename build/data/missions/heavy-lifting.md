Pick up the hard drive from the storage locker and run it to Raul's houseboat, then take the boat down to Ajax Gym. As Jason, take the tour; as Lucia, change into gym gear, search a bag in the locker room and corner your target in the tanning area long enough to read his phone.

Come back at night and tail the crew by car. Hang back, follow them into the Grand Majestic Aparthotel and ride up to the roof.
