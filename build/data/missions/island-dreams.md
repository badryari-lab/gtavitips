You drop in at Kenton's shop, drive his boat out to the dive spot and suit up at the stern. Lead the tour group through an underwater trench to the wreck.

While they look around, switch to the detector, follow the signal and scan near the hull. When a diver gets stuck, swim in and lift him free. On the way back up, keep the group safe from what comes out of the blue, then drive everyone back to the dock.
