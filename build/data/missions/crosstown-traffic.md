Pick a side. As Jason, ride a bike along the river paths and keep the boats in sight. As Lucia, chase down the train, board the rear car and lean on the man at the front until he makes the call.

Then it is a river fight. Clear the dock, jump onto the target boat, stop the train as Lucia and defend the boat from gang crews, jet skis, the Coast Guard and a helicopter until the chase breaks.
