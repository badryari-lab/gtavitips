You drive to the search area in the swamp, ask the fisherman on the dock what he saw and check the package under the roof by the shore. Then take the boat to the shack he points at.

Clear out the crew on the deck, grab the product off the cooler inside and deal with the airboat that rolls up. Run it all the way back to Key Lento and drop it in the box by the trailer.
