You meet Cal on the causeway and drive his truck to Hamlet. He loads a cop scanner onto your phone, then you climb into the impound and work your way through the lot to the red Imponte Phoenix.

Hotwire it, get out and shake the police. They still have the car's description, so run it through a Pay 'n' Spray, park it in Brian's garage and collect. To finish, swap your hat, glasses and outfit so the law stops recognizing you.
