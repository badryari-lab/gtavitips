Pack up the last bags at the old apartment, get on the road and follow the marker across the bridge to the Y Hotel on Ocean Beach. Leave the ride with the valet and head inside.

Meet your host in the lobby, take the elevator to the seventh floor and walk down to suite 701. Look around the place with Lucia, raid the wardrobe if you want a new fit, and bring her a glass of champagne out on the balcony to wrap it up.
