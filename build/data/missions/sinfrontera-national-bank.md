Go to the bank in Tequesta, climb the steps and follow Jason to a table in the hall. Use focus and hold inspect on each security feature: the cameras over the counter, the man in the pink suit, the two guards by the lifts and the panel between them.

When Raul calls, head upstairs and take the seat next to him.
