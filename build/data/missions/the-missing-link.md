Go alone as Lucia to Sloppy Molly's in Watson Bay. Follow Andrea across to the warehouse, get past the door and find the man at the back of the cockfight.

Pose for the photos, knock him out and fight your way to the car. Lose the gang on the road, switch to Jason at the site, walk him to the backyard and work the loader while Raul asks the questions.
