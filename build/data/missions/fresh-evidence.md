Meet Dre'Quan at Jack of Hearts and drive out to Starfish Island. After dark, climb into the mansion, deal with the guard in the gym quietly and search the upstairs for a key card.

Work your way down past patrols and a roaming tiger, open the basement surveillance room, copy the files, then fight back out to the dock and lose the cops on the water.
