Drive to Venture Apartments in Rockridge and park outside the red area. Ask for Raymond in the lobby and knock on the red door upstairs.

When the raid starts, grab the bag and fight floor by floor through the building. Help Lucia down from a window, pick Jason up in a car, lose the police and meet Boobie outside the Jack of Hearts.
