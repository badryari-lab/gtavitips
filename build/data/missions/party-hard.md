Meet the client at the airport and follow the convoy out, with a detour to Little Cuba for his drink order. Drive downtown to the Megamundo tower and follow the group up in the elevator.

When the night turns, play both sides. As Jason, clear the conference room and the hallway and push up the stairs. As Lucia, sneak to the stairwell, crawl through the vent and clear the party floor without hitting anyone you are there to save. Finish as Jason on the roof and land the helicopter on the helipad.
