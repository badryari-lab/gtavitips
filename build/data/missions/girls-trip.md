Ask the people around the motel about painkillers until you find Vicki, buy what she has and take it to Jason. When that does not work, go back to Vicki and get in her car.

Drive her around Port Gellhorn: a stop at the club, the boatyard, then the car wash where you have to deal with the Kolts. Chase Dunk down when he runs, and drive him home to his trailer.
