Ask at the hotel front desk for messages, then drive to Ocean Beach Marina and search the houseboat at the end of the pier.

When the police hit the docks, fight back along the walkways to a fast boat, hold it while the engine starts and survive a chase through the bay and a canal. Ditch the boat between two barges and stay out of sight.
