You drive to the Ammu-Nation in Key Lento and talk to Andrew at the counter. Pick any longarm you can afford: shotgun, rifle, whatever fits the cash in your pocket. Short on money, you leave and come back later.

Once the gun is yours, the mission walks you through how stock grows as you buy and where to stash weapons. Then you step outside, and you need that new purchase straight away.
