You start as Jason at the Rusty Anchor: order a drink, meet the group by the pier and drive the speedboat through the city's waterways to the yacht. Then race the jet ski back through the channels.

As Lucia, shoot champagne bottles out of the air with focus, pick a dress and search the decks for Mattia. Keep up with his car through the tunnels into downtown and finish on the dance floor.
