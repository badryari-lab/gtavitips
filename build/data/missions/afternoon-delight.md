Start as Lucia at the front and talk to Vicki at the bar. Switch to Jason, find the way in round the back and answer the favor in the dressing room.

Watch the show as Lucia until Jason makes his move. Then rob the place: the office safe through the kitchen, the registers at the bar. Shoot your way out past security, lose the bikers on the road and meet Vicki.
