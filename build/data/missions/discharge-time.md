You start as Lucia outside the hospital at night. Get past the front desk, take the stairs to the second floor and find Jason's room. From there control swaps between the two of you: Jason shoots from the wheelchair while Lucia pushes, then Lucia fights hand to hand and with whatever guns drop.

The last stretch puts you in the back of an ambulance with the doors open. Lucia drives, you shoot, and the mission ends when nothing is left behind you.
