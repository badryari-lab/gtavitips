You talk to Brian at the bar, taxi the seaplane into the water and take off. Fly to the intercept point and hold position just above Pilot Pete's plane so the radar sees one aircraft.

Peel off to the next airstrip, land and taxi to the hangar. When the shooting starts, clear the hangar, the offices and the yard, then hold the line by the water tanks. Finish by grabbing a motorbike and racing down the runway to the plane.
