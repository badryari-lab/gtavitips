You walk into the boutique and ask the clerk for Brian's money. When that goes nowhere, search the place: the register, the back office, then a keypad door.

Find the code, get inside, grab what is on the bench and sweep the shelves. Call Brian, walk next door to his lockup, punch in the code and leave the bag in the strongbox.
