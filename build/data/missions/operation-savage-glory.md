Plant charges on a bridge pillar as Lucia, then switch to Jason to clear a cargo ship and rig its containers with a sniffer dog leading the way. Raise the second bridge, swim through a tunnel to reach the coast guard base and find the seized load.

Cover the loading, take out the RPG gunner, attach the winch to the helicopter and ride the cargo net out across the city with a rifle.
