You drive to the prison in Hamlet, check in at reception and wait at the visitors' window. Once she is out, walk her to the truck and take the long road south to the Rusty Anchor.

Inside, buy the drinks, work the room, dance, play a few rounds of pool and settle a fistfight in the parking lot. When the sirens start, stay on Lucia's heels down to the beach.
