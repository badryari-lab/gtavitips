Talk to Nicole at Raul's compound in Stockyard and take the truck to Gil's mansion in Peacock Estates. As Jason, sneak in, play along in the backyard, then find the remote in Gil's office.

Attach the winch, pull the safe out of the window as Lucia and haul it west to Felipe, using it to smash pursuers. Back at the compound, drill, thread the cable and hold the door to crack it.
