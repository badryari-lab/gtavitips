San4San has been in [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) longer than most. **The crew is tied into the city's music scene and its drug trade, and in this town those overlap.**

Their ground is La Perle and the north of the city. They run five Illegal Operations, the most of any Vice City gang. Illegal Operations are gang hideouts you can rob for cash and product. Clean one out and it restocks after a while.

Two of them hold unique weapons. The Sound4Sound Studio in Stockyard has the San4San K17, a version of the [Klose K17](/gameplay/weapons/klose-k17). Janjak's Garage in La Perle has the San4San KZ-59, a version of the [Kozlov KZ-59](/gameplay/weapons/kozlov-kz-59).
