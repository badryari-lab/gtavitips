San 4 San has territory in [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city). The name suggests a founding count or a code or a specific incident that four people survived together. The actual origin is not confirmed in official sources. The name is the name and the gang operates under it across established Vice City districts.

The territorial model is conventional by Vice City street gang standards. Hold the blocks, control the corners, manage the supply relationship from above, and handle the competition from the adjacent crews who want the same territory. **[Real Dimez](/gameplay/gangs-and-factions/real-dimez) and [7X](/gameplay/gangs-and-factions/7x) are both in the same operational environment.** The competition between all three is structural to the Vice City street economy.

San 4 San's confirmed presence in both trailers places them as a visible part of the Vice City street landscape rather than a background faction. They are the kind of crew the protagonist will interact with, work alongside, work against, or navigate around depending on where the mission structure takes the story.
