The Leonida Highway Patrol specializes in the road environment that most of Leonida's criminal transport uses. The highway network between [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) and the state interior is the supply chain corridor for everything that moves by road. LHP patrols it.

The pursuit implications are mechanical. Highway pursuit is a different problem from urban pursuit. Long sight lines, high-speed capable vehicles on both sides, and fewer of the urban environment features that allow an escaping vehicle to create distance through maneuvering rather than speed. **Getting onto the highway in front of LHP units requires a vehicle that can hold the advantage for the distance it takes to exit the network.**

LHP and Leonida State Police have overlapping jurisdiction on the major road network. The practical difference is specialization: [State Police](/gameplay/gangs-and-factions/leonida-state-police) cover the full rural territory, LHP focuses on the highway corridors specifically. Both agencies respond to the same high-speed incidents on the road network.
