Real Dimez works the blocks that [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city)'s tourism economy is built on but does not acknowledge. The residential districts behind the beachfront strip have their own economy running parallel to the hotel and club layer above them. **Real Dimez is part of that economy at the street level.**

The operation is territorial rather than logistical. Real Dimez controls corners and blocks rather than supply chains. They buy from above and sell below. The margin is the business. The territory is what makes the margin possible and what every other street crew in the same zip code wants.

Interactions with [7X](/gameplay/gangs-and-factions/7x) are a regular part of the Vice City street environment. The two crews operate in overlapping geography with different organizational models. Real Dimez has enough structure to maintain territory. 7X has enough numbers to contest it periodically. The friction between those two facts is ongoing.
