Kolt & Associates run [Port Gellhorn](/gameplay/locations/port-gellhorn)'s pill trade. **Most of the illegal painkillers on the Gulf Coast go through them, and the name on the door makes it sound like a law firm.**

Their ground is Port Gellhorn and the [Kelly County](/gameplay/locations/kelly-county) coast around it. They run five Illegal Operations, as many as any gang in Leonida. Illegal Operations are gang hideouts you can rob for cash and product. Clean one out and it restocks after a while.

The Stash House in Port Gellhorn is the one with a prize: the Kolt & Associates 556, a unique version of the [Duke 556](/gameplay/weapons/duke-556).
