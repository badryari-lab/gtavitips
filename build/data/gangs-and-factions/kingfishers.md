The Kingfishers were born in the [Grassrivers](/gameplay/locations/grassrivers) and never left. **Drugs and contraband come in off the coast, and the swamp is the road nobody else can drive.**

Their ground runs around Cottonmouth Lake and down toward [Key Lento](/gameplay/locations/key-lento). They run three Illegal Operations. Illegal Operations are gang hideouts you can rob for cash and product. Clean one out and it restocks after a while.

Two of them hold unique weapons. The Manatee Motel in Gullet Marsh has the Kingfishers Laird 350, a version of the [Fortune & Son Laird 350](/gameplay/weapons/fortune-and-son-laird-350). The Sunrise RV Park, north of Cottonmouth Lake, has the Kingfishers Akulus, a version of the [Scorpio Akulus](/gameplay/weapons/scorpio-akulus).
