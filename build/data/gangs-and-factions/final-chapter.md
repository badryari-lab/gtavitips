Final Chapter is the motorcycle club of [Ambrosia](/gameplay/locations/ambrosia). The Allied Crystal sugar refinery gives the town its jobs; the club gives it almost everything else. **In public they ride for charity under the flag. In private they cook and sell meth.**

Their ground is Ambrosia and the farm country of [Kelly County](/gameplay/locations/kelly-county) around it. The club runs four Illegal Operations, the Farm Fix Meth Lab and Hog Stars Garage among them. Illegal Operations are gang hideouts you can rob for cash and product. Clean one out and it restocks after a while.

They do not stay out of the story either. Jason and Lucia run into the bikers more than once, and rarely on friendly terms.
