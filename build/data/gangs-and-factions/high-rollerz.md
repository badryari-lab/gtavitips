The High Rollerz control [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city)'s illegal racing circuit. Not one track, not one location, but the network of roads, times, and arrangements that make the racing possible. **The circuit moves. The crew manages where it goes.**

The vehicle culture is the foundation. Modified performance cars, import builds, domestic muscle with engine work that the insurance documents do not reflect. The High Rollerz know who has what, who is fast, and what the current betting lines are on any given night. This knowledge is worth more than the racing itself.

Vice City provides the geography the High Rollerz need. The industrial waterfront at night has stretches that are long enough and empty enough for the quarter-mile runs. The highway segments on the Vice County approach have the elevation changes that make certain runs memorable. The beachfront strips have the audience. All three are in the High Rollerz operational territory.
