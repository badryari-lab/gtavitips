La Polilla Negra, the Black Moth, came out of Colombia's wars: former paramilitaries who went into the drug trade. **They use Leonida as their way into the United States, and they do not do street corners.**

Their ground is Watson Bay. The cartel runs two Illegal Operations there. Illegal Operations are gang hideouts you can rob for cash and product. Clean one out and it restocks after a while.

One of them is the Palmer Fishing Warehouse, and it holds La Polilla Negra KZ-59, a unique version of the [Kozlov KZ-59](/gameplay/weapons/kozlov-kz-59). Bring enough to take it.
