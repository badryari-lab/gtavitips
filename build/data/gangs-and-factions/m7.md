M7 is a Salvadoran gang, and you can read it on their faces. **Face tattoos, extortion, meth, and violence that makes the first two easier.**

Their ground is [Hamlet](/gameplay/locations/hamlet), with one operation far from home: a weed grow on Lake Tallaloko in [Lummox County](/gameplay/locations/lummox-county). Two Illegal Operations in all. Illegal Operations are gang hideouts you can rob for cash and product. Clean one out and it restocks after a while.

Both hold weapons stamped Marabunta Grande. The Nova Auto Junkyard in Hamlet has the Marabunta Grande KZ-59, a version of the [Kozlov KZ-59](/gameplay/weapons/kozlov-kz-59). The Weed Grow has the Marabunta Grande Type 9, a version of the [Zhang Wu Type 9](/gameplay/weapons/zhang-wu-type-9). The junkyard also hides a [Bravado Gauntlet Hellfire](/gameplay/vehicles/bravado-gauntlet-hellfire) that Randy wants.
