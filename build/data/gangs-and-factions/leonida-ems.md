Leonida EMS runs the ambulance and paramedic infrastructure across the state. The [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) call volume is what you would expect from a dense urban environment with an active nightlife economy, a tourist population that makes poor decisions in warm weather, and a criminal landscape that generates a specific category of medical emergency at predictable hours.

The rural and Keys coverage is the harder operational challenge. A medical emergency in the outer [Leonida Keys](/gameplay/locations/leonida-keys) or in the [Grassrivers](/gameplay/locations/grassrivers) interior has a response time that reflects the geography. **The EMS unit that covers Port Gellhorn is not the EMS unit that covers the Vice City tourist strip.** Both agencies carry the same name. The response time difference is measured in minutes that matter.

In Leonida, EMS vehicles appear at incident scenes as part of the ambient world response. The ambulance that shows up after a confrontation is the EMS infrastructure doing its job. The paramedics working the Vice City waterfront at two in the morning are the same agency working a highway accident on the [Vice County](/gameplay/locations/vice-county) corridor at six. Same trucks, different call types, same uniform.
