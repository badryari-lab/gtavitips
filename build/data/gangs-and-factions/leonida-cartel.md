The Leonida Cartel operates the supply chain. Not the street corner, not the retail transaction, not the collection of debts from individual buyers. **The cartel is the wholesale layer.** The product arrives at [Port Gellhorn](/gameplay/locations/port-gellhorn), moves through the [Vice County](/gameplay/locations/vice-county) warehouse and logistics infrastructure, and reaches the distribution network that the street gangs operate.

[Raul Bautista](/gameplay/characters/raul-bautista)'s connection to the [Port Gellhorn](/gameplay/locations/port-gellhorn) corridor places him in the cartel's operational geography. The database entry on Bautista confirms his presence in the port zone and his role as a product mover in the supply chain. The cartel does not surface individual names easily. Bautista is an exception that confirms the rule.

The cartel's relationship with law enforcement is managed rather than avoided. The scale of the operation requires some part of the institutional infrastructure to be aware of it and to have made a calculation about what the arrangement is worth. That calculation is ongoing.
