The Leonida Department of Corrections runs the state prison system. In Leonida this agency is not background scenery. The story opens with [Lucia Caminos](/gameplay/characters/lucia-caminos) incarcerated. The DOC is the institution she is held in, the environment the opening sequence establishes, and the system the story begins inside.

**Prisoner transport, facility management, and the security apparatus of a state correctional system** are the DOC's operational profile. In Leonida this translates to specific mission types: transport interdiction, facility infiltration, and the story beats that involve navigating the institutional machinery of the criminal justice system rather than avoiding it.

The real Florida correctional system has a specific character that the design will have referenced. Large facilities, significant staffing, and the specific institutional culture that develops in environments built around long-term detention. The Leonida DOC will carry enough of that character to be recognizable.
