The Guardia Brothers are a Cuban crime family, several generations deep. **Drugs, robbery, kidnapping, politics: in [Little Cuba](/gameplay/locations/little-cuba) there is not much the family does not touch.**

Their ground is Little Cuba in [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city), and their house is Casa Guardia. They run three Illegal Operations. Illegal Operations are gang hideouts you can rob for cash and product. Clean one out and it restocks after a while.

Casa Guardia itself holds the Guardia Brothers FRA, a unique version of the [AdB FRA](/gameplay/weapons/adb-fra).
