The Leonida State Police are the law enforcement layer that covers the parts of the state that VCPD does not own. That is most of the state by area and a significant portion of it by population. The highway network, the [Vice County](/gameplay/locations/vice-county) rural zones, the approaches to the Keys, the road past [Port Gellhorn](/gameplay/locations/port-gellhorn) into the interior. All of it is State Police territory.

The response model reflects the geography. On the [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) approach highways, State Police units are present and visible. Move into the rural interior and the coverage becomes predictable in its pattern rather than in its density. **The [OMG](/gameplay/gangs-and-factions/outlaw-motorcycle-gang) knows this. The [cartel](/gameplay/gangs-and-factions/leonida-cartel) supply chain knows this. Everyone who moves product along the Vice County corridor has the State Police patrol schedule built into their operational timing.**

Wanted level interactions with the State Police reflect their jurisdiction. In the city, [VCPD](/gameplay/gangs-and-factions/vcpd) arrives first. On the highway out of the city, State Police units take over. The transition between the two agencies is one of the mechanical details that Leonida uses to communicate where you are relative to its law enforcement infrastructure.
