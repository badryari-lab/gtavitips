NOOSE is the wanted-level ceiling in Leonida. Anyone who has reached the upper end of the wanted system knows what this agency represents: the point at which the situation has moved beyond municipal law enforcement and into federal tactical response territory.

The deployment threshold means NOOSE encounters are by definition the most intense law enforcement situations in Leonida. You do not encounter NOOSE because of a petty crime. You encounter NOOSE because the situation escalated through multiple levels of response and each level failed to terminate it. **By the time NOOSE arrives, the situation has been ongoing long enough to generate a significant response budget.**

NOOSE equipment and tactics reflect the mandate. Armored vehicles, tactical weapons, and the training profile that comes from an agency that exists specifically to handle situations that standard police units cannot. The Leonida NOOSE operates within the same framework:ries, adapted to the Leonida context.
