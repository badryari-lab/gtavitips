Binco is where you go when the priority is clothes and the budget is the constraint. Every [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) strip mall with enough square footage has one. The store layout is identical across all locations: racks, bins, a changing room with a curtain that does not fully close.

**The clothes are functional.** They fit the body in the way clothes are supposed to fit the body. They wash without incident. They do not communicate wealth, status, or aspiration. They communicate that the person wearing them got dressed this morning, which is a more common communication goal than the fashion industry acknowledges.

The Leonida protagonist will spend time in Binco. Not because it is the preferred destination but because it is on the route to everywhere else and the price of entry is the lowest in the state. First wardrobe after losing everything. Quick replacement when the current item is no longer suitable for continued wear. Binco handles both situations without comment.
