Obey has been in the GTA universe since GTA IV and has consistently occupied the performance sedan tier. In GTA 6, the Tailgater continues that position. The Omnis E-GT is the new development: a confirmed electric performance vehicle in Leonida.

The Omnis E-GT is significant because it confirms that the GTA 6 vehicle roster includes contemporary EV technology. **In a city like [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city), where conspicuous consumption is the default cultural mode, driving an electric performance car is either a statement about values or a statement about how the owner wants to be perceived.** Both readings are valid in Leonida.

The Tailgater is the working vehicle. Quick, composed, and capable of operating across the full range of Vice City conditions without attracting unnecessary attention from anyone who is not specifically looking for it.
