Ron is a city brand. **All seven of its stations sit in Vice-Dale County: Southside, Salton, two in Balsero, [Little Cuba](/gameplay/locations/little-cuba), Bayside and [Washington Beach](/gameplay/locations/washington-beach).** Out in the counties you fill up somewhere else.

Every vehicle burns fuel. Stop beside a pump until Services appears: Refuel fills the tank and shows the price first, Repair patches the car up for a fee.

Stations appear as a blue pump once the fog clears. Vehicles, then Gas Stations, lists every one you have found.
