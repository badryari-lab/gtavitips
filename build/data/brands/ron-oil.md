Ron Oil is the competition. Where Global Oil has the industrial waterfront and the institutional accounts, Ron Oil has the highway junctions where price-sensitive drivers choose the orange pump over the Global pump based on a difference of cents per gallon.

The brand name implies a founder called Ron. No Ron has been identified in any public filing associated with the company. **The name is either a tribute to someone who no longer needs to be named, or it is the company that needed a name and Ron was available.** The stations run either way.

In gameplay terms Ron Oil stations appear on the [Vice County](/gameplay/locations/vice-county) road network as rest stops, vehicle refueling points, and the kind of location where the person behind the counter has seen something they are not going to report. Both things are true of every real highway gas station and Ron Oil is the correct brand for that specific atmosphere.
