FlyUS is the airline at every airport in Leonida. The flag carrier of the in-world skies. **The blue and white livery is as recognizable in the series as the Weazel News helicopter or the Lifeguard truck.**

In Leonida, FlyUS operates out of [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) International on the domestic and international network. The premium cabin exists. The economy cabin is significantly larger than the premium cabin, which is the structure of every airline regardless of what the advertising suggests.

The FlyUS aircraft appear throughout the airport sequences in the official footage. The brand is present at check-in, on the tarmac, and in the departure gate signage. It is ambient infrastructure for any Vice City airport mission and will function as the protagonist's air transport for missions that require leaving Leonida.
