Azteca Gold's vans move through [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) the way delivery fleets always do: parked at an angle nobody would choose on purpose, hazards on, driver already halfway to the next stop before the last package hits the doorstep.

**The name says gold, the operation says volume.** Whatever's actually in the back of the van is less interesting than the schedule that gets it there. Delivery brands don't sell products so much as they sell the promise that the thing will show up, and Azteca Gold's trucks are exactly the kind of street furniture that promise looks like in practice.

No storefront, no confirmed warehouse, nothing beyond the van itself moving through traffic. It's infrastructure painted to look like a company, which is most of what a delivery brand actually is.
