Post OP operates in the space that GoPostal leaves open, which is not much space but enough to sustain a logistics operation in a state the size of Leonida. **The rivalry between GoPostal and Post OP plays out in [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) parking lots and narrow [Vice County](/gameplay/locations/vice-county) roads where both vans are trying to make the same delivery window.**

The Post OP brand positioning is professional. The uniforms are pressed. The vehicles are maintained to a higher visible standard than GoPostal. Whether the delivery times are better is a question that Vice County consumer affairs has been reviewing for two years without a published finding.

In Leonida, Post OP vans serve the same functions as GoPostal vans with a different livery. The cargo is variable. The driver has a schedule. The schedule is always an inconvenience to whoever needs the van for something else.
