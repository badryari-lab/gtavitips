Nagasaki builds for the parts of Leonida that other manufacturers did not design their vehicles to reach. The Blazer ATV is the standard configuration. The Blazer Lifeguard is the same platform in emergency service configuration, confirming both beach patrol and interior deployment.

The Dinghy is the marine entry. A rigid inflatable boat confirmed in coastal sequences across official footage. **The Carbon RS and the Outlaw extend the range into performance motorcycle and off-road specialist territory.**

Five confirmed models across land and water. No other manufacturer in Leonida covers this range. When the protagonist leaves the road, they are statistically likely to be on a Nagasaki product.
