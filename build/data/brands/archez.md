Archez is on every commercial strip in Leonida that has a road and a power supply. The signage is brown. The hat logo is a cowboy hat because the brand wants you to associate processed meat with the frontier, which is either genius or deranged, and the franchise has not decided which.

The core product is the roast beef sandwich. Everything else on the menu is architecture around that decision. The curly fries have a following that Archez corporate has never been able to explain or replicate on purpose. **They just kept making them and the market responded.**

The [Vice County](/gameplay/locations/vice-county) locations do brisk late-afternoon business from the construction and logistics workers on the corridor between Port Gellhorn and the inland. Nobody is choosing Archez. They are choosing speed, proximity, and the path of least resistance. Archez is all three of those things simultaneously.
