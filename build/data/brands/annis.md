Annis does not advertise to people who need convincing. The cars sell themselves to the people who already know what they are looking at. The Elegy Retro Custom is a heritage build dressed as a road car. The Hellion is an off-road buggy with a sports car engine dropped in the back.

Both confirmed in Leonida. Both confirmed as the kind of vehicle that attracts attention from other drivers before it attracts attention from law enforcement. **The street takeover circuit in [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) runs a significant percentage of Annis metal.**

The modification potential is the point. The factory specification is the starting position, not the destination.
