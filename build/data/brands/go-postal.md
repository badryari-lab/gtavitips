GoPostal is the most visible logistics brand in Leonida. The yellow vans are on every road in [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) at every hour of the day. The density of GoPostal vehicles on Vice City streets is a public infrastructure question that the city has chosen not to formally investigate.

**The van is the brand.** Not the logo, not the drivers, not the tracking system that generates updates describing package locations in terms that are accurate but not helpful. The yellow van in traffic is the GoPostal brand experience, and the brand has that experience on every street in the state.

In GTA missions, the GoPostal van has been a target, a getaway vehicle, a cover identity, and a problem. In Leonida it will serve all of these functions again. The driver has a route. The route is not your concern. The van is.
