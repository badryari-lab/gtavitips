Global Oil is the dominant energy brand in Leonida. The refineries are in [Vice County](/gameplay/locations/vice-county) on the industrial waterfront, which is where refineries are in every state that has them. The gas station network runs from [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) to the [Keys](/gameplay/locations/leonida-keys), which means there is a Global Oil pump at every point where a Leonida road reaches a practical end.

The environmental review situation is ongoing. The Vice County industrial corridor has a relationship with the water downstream that the local authority has been attempting to characterize accurately for some time. **The refinery keeps operating while the characterization continues.**

In gameplay the Global Oil infrastructure serves as mission locations. The refinery is the kind of facility where things happen that require entering a large industrial complex. The gas stations are the roadside ambient markers of how far into Vice County you currently are.
