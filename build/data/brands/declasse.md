Declasse is the background car manufacturer of Leonida. The sedan in the parking lot. The SUV in the school run. The vehicle that ends up in every wide shot of a [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) street because it is statistically the most common car in the state.

The brand name is French for low-class. The brand was named for the demographics it sells to and that demographic buys the cars without complaint, which is either the most practical relationship between a brand and its customers in Leonida or the most cynical. **Probably both.**

In gameplay terms, Declasse vehicles are the cars you get into when you need a car and the faster options are not nearby. They are competent. They are available. They do not communicate anything about the driver except that the driver needed a car and this was the car. In Leonida, that is most of the driving population.
