Cluckin' Bell solved a problem nobody had articulated by combining fried chicken and Mexican fast food into one building and putting a rooster on the front. The name is the joke. The commercial operation underneath it is not. **It is the second-largest fast food chain in Leonida by location count.**

The chicken is identifiable. The other menu items operate in a category that requires a different kind of commitment from the customer. The combination works because both halves of the menu are aimed at the same person at the same moment: hungry, near a Cluckin' Bell, not interested in deliberating.

The [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) locations run twenty-three hours. The one hour of closure per day is used for cleaning. Whether the cleaning is sufficient is a question the county health department revisits quarterly. The answer has been consistent enough to keep the locations operational.
