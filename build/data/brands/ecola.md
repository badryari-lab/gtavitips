eCola is the default carbonated option in Leonida. Not by reputation. Not by advertising. By presence. It is in every cooler in every establishment that has electricity and a flat surface. Vending machines in parking structures. Fridges behind gas station counters at three in the morning. **The distribution network is the product.**

The formula has never been publicly disclosed. The regulatory filings describe the ingredients as proprietary natural and artificial flavors, which is a sentence that does not describe anything while technically describing something. The color is immediately recognizable. The effect of consuming it in quantity is documented in [Vice County](/gameplay/locations/vice-county) emergency services data.

The rivalry with Sprunk is structural. Wherever eCola has a vending machine, Sprunk has a vending machine next to it. Wherever Sprunk sponsors an event, eCola sponsors a competing event on the same weekend. **This has been the dynamic for as long as either brand has operated in Leonida.**
