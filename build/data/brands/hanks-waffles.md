There is one Hank's Waffles, and it is out in [Kelly County](/gameplay/locations/kelly-county). **The city has its own waffle house; this one belongs to the farm roads.**

A plate gives Jason or Lucia a short boost to their stats. Fast food shows on the map as a blue burger once the fog clears; Food & Drink, then Fast Food, lists what you have found.
