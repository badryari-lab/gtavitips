OnlyBangs is the Leonida parody of the creator economy's adult tier. In [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city), the platform is part of the economic landscape in the same way that real adult content platforms are part of the real Miami economic landscape: **present, significant, and rarely discussed in polite company while being discussed constantly in every other context.**

The brand appears in the game world as advertising, as a character income source, as the thing someone is filming when you walk past them on the Vice City beachfront, and as the reason certain Vice City properties have better lighting than the building quality would suggest is warranted.

Leonida has always included adult industry parody as part of the Vice City economic texture. OnlyBangs updates this to the current platform model. The commentary is the same as it has always been: the city sells itself and the platform takes a percentage.
