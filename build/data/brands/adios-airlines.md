Adios Airlines is the carrier you book when the price is the only factor. The seat is a seat. The legroom is the quantity of legroom that results from charging less for the seat. The carry-on policy has generated more [Vice County](/gameplay/locations/vice-county) consumer complaints than any other airline practice in Leonida.

**The name is Spanish for goodbye.** The airline named itself goodbye. Whether this is ironic commentary on the budget airline experience or an oversight from the founding board has not been clarified. The name has been retained across multiple rebranding reviews.

The [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) International routes to destinations within Leonida and to the neighboring states are Adios's core network. The aircraft are operational. The destinations are reached. The experience between departure and arrival is the cost of the price and every Adios passenger understands this before they book.
