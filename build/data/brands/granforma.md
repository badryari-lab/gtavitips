Granforma builds the Zorrusso, and that badge alone puts it at the top of Randy Kozlov's list. **The first car he puts a price on wears it.**

The [Zorrusso](/gameplay/vehicles/granforma-zorrusso) is a mid-engine supercar that looks fast parked. In Chapter 3 it waits in the Hathaway Apartments garage in [Ocean Beach](/gameplay/locations/ocean-beach), and Randy has a buyer for it on [his first job](/story/missions/clonin-keys-makin-gs). Clone the key, keep the paint clean, deliver it.
