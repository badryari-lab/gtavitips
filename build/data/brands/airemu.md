AirEmu is the middle option in Leonida air travel. The aircraft are maintained to standard. The seats are the seats that came with the aircraft. The food is the food that comes in the sealed plastic tray. **The emu on the tail of every aircraft is the most interesting thing about the brand and it cannot fly.**

The mid-market position means AirEmu gets the customer who looked at Adios and decided the price difference was worth it, and the customer who looked at FlyUS and decided the price difference was not. This is a large segment of the Leonida traveling population and AirEmu has served it without distinction for years.

In Leonida the AirEmu aircraft are part of the [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) International airport environment. The domestic network connects the key Leonida population centers. Whether the player interacts with AirEmu as a functional travel option or as background aviation furniture depends on the mission structure.
