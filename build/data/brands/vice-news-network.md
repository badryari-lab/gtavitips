Vice News Network is [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city)'s local news channel. Where Weazel News covers Leonida from a position of editorial authority, VNN covers Vice City from a position of proximity. **The helicopter is always above the correct building. The reporter is always at the correct intersection.** This is either excellent journalism or something else.

The VNN ticker covers local [Vice County](/gameplay/locations/vice-county) crime, local Vice City events, and the specific category of Leonida news that the national networks do not pick up because it is too small or too specific or too honest about what is actually happening in the state.

In gameplay VNN serves as the local news ambient media layer. The reports reference missions that the player has completed, the current wanted level situation in Vice City, and the ongoing narrative of whatever crisis is developing in Leonida at any given moment. The reporters are enthusiastic about all of it.
