Schyster is the American answer to Benefactor. Where Benefactor communicates European discretion, Schyster communicates American confidence. The PMP 700 is a large executive sedan built to the proportions that suggest its owner does not need to look for parking because parking finds them.

In Leonida, the Schyster occupies a specific social tier: the [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) businessman who made their money in a way that is technically legal and wants a vehicle that reflects both the money and the technicality. **The PMP 700 has the visual presence of a vehicle that expects to be waved through.**

One confirmed model. The PMP 700 is not trying to be a range. It is trying to be the definitive version of one thing. In Leonida, that one thing is an American executive car with an attitude problem.
