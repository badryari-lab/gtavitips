One-Eyed Willie's is the off-road shop of Leonida. **It sits in north [Leonard County](/gameplay/locations/leonard-county), just above [Lake Leonida](/gameplay/locations/lake-leonida), and it opens in Chapter 1.**

Bring something that is meant to leave the road. Willie's does off-road modifications and hand-painted liveries, and many of them are sold nowhere else.

Set a waypoint from the map: Vehicles, then Mod Shops. The shop is only open for business with the Ultimate Edition.
