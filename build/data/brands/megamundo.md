MegaMundo is opening its first American corporate headquarters, and it's doing it loudly. The tower carries "MEGAMUNDO" lettering up its face, a rainbow-arch emblem over the entrance, and a green "MEGA MUNDO" banner strung above the staircase where the opening-night speech gets delivered. This is a company that wants the skyline to know it arrived.

**Leadership runs through two very different registers.** [Petra Navarro](/gameplay/characters/petra-navarro) is the COO, composed and direct, the one who wants Andres watched all night and on time for the speeches. [Andres de Leon](/gameplay/characters/andres-de-leon) is the network's telenovela star and her husband, out front for the speech itself, fast-talking and volatile enough that the security brief for him is to keep him in line as much as to protect him.

The opening event is staffed like a real corporate launch: door security screening every guest, [Gustavo Herrera](/gameplay/characters/gustavo) running security for the Navarros, hired protection in [Jason Duval](/gameplay/characters/jason-duval) and [Lucia Caminos](/gameplay/characters/lucia-caminos) circulating the floor. Opening night is supposed to be about the network, the tower and the cameras.
