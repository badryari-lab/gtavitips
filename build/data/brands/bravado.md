Bravado is the American manufacturer in Leonida. Not American by heritage or country of registration. American by philosophy: bigger displacement, higher ride height, louder exhaust note than the engineering requires.

The pickup truck range dominates [Vice County](/gameplay/locations/vice-county). The farms, the construction sites, the people who need something that will pull a trailer and survive being used as a trailer itself. **The Bravado pickup is so common in Vice County that the absence of one is the unusual observation.**

On [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city)'s strips, the Bravado muscle car line takes a different role. Lower, louder, more chrome than function requires. The person driving it made a choice and the choice was Bravado, which in Vice City communicates something specific about the driver's relationship with subtlety.
