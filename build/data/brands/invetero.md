Invetero is the American luxury manufacturer. Not the flashiest option in Leonida. Not the fastest. **The choice of the person who does not need the car to announce itself because the car has been making the same announcement for long enough that the announcement is taken for granted.**

The [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) Invetero is usually the car in the long driveway of the property on the Intracoastal. The one that belongs to whoever owns the property. Not a new purchase. A continuation. The Invetero you buy because your father had one and his father had one and the brand has never given you a reason to reconsider.

In gameplay terms the Invetero appears in the upper-end NPC vehicle pool and as mission-specific vehicles for characters who communicate wealth through understatement rather than volume. It is the car that tells you something about who owns it without the car itself doing anything particularly loud.
