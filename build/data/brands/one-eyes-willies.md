Leonida is not all coastal highway. The state has inland terrain, Grassrivers, the back roads off Mount Kalaga, everything that doesn't have a lane marking. One-Eyes Willies is the shop for drivers who treat those conditions as a destination, not a problem to route around.

Suspension lifts, skid plates, all-terrain tire packages. The kind of modifications that mean something when the pavement runs out. The [Vapid Dominator Buggy](/gameplay/vehicles/vapid-dominator-buggy) is built for exactly this kind of use, and a shop like One-Eyes Willies is where you push it further.

**Ultimate Edition access only.** Rockstar has confirmed the shop as part of the June 2026 pre-order package. Specific services, pricing, and whether there's a physical location or a menu-based system have not been detailed yet.
