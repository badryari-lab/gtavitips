Dinka is the background noise of Leonida traffic. The Blista Compact is on every commercial strip and residential block in [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city). It is the vehicle of delivery drivers, students, and anyone who needs to move through the city without attracting comment.

The Double-T is the motorcycle variant of the same philosophy. Compact, efficient, and quick enough through Vice City traffic to be genuinely useful. **Both vehicles have confirmed appearances across multiple sequences in official footage.**

Dinka does not build the car you want. It builds the car you need. In a city where most of the interesting vehicles end up in a canal, that distinction matters more than it sounds.
