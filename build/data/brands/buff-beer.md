Buff Beer positions itself at the junction of fitness culture and the decision to drink beer after fitness culture. The can design features a bicep. **The bicep on the can is more defined than the average bicep in the average [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) gym.** This aspirational gap is the product.

The sponsorship portfolio is ambitious and consistent. Beach volleyball, boat shows, a Vice City half-marathon whose finish line finishes at a Buff Beer tent. The marketing department has concluded that pre-workout and post-workout are the same customer at different points in the same day.

The beer is a light lager. Nothing about the flavor justifies the intensity of the brand identity. This is intentional. **The product is the can. The identity the can projects is what you are actually paying for.** The liquid inside is secondary and Buff Beer has known this since the brand launched.
