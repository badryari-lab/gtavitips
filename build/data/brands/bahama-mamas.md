Bahama Mamas is back. It returns in a [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) that has grown around it. **The exterior is the same. The interior has been renovated. The queue has not changed.**

The club operates at the intersection of Vice City's legitimate nightlife economy and the less legitimate economy that uses the nightlife as infrastructure. This has been the case since the original location opened. The ownership structure has been reviewed multiple times by multiple agencies. The club has remained operational throughout.

In gameplay Bahama Mamas functions as a mission location, a meeting point, and an environment that the game uses to demonstrate what Vice City nightlife looks like at full capacity. The bar is staffed, the floor is occupied, and something is happening in the VIP section that will become relevant later.
