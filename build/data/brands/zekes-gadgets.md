The sign at Zeke's Gadgets says "We Do Repairs," no branding beyond the promise itself. That's the whole business: the kind of storefront that exists on every third block in [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city), fixing the phones and radios people would rather patch than replace.

**Somebody walks in armed.** Whatever business that customer had, it's the kind that doesn't get explained at the counter. Repair shops in this state of the country have a long, quiet tradition of being more than they advertise. Zeke's doesn't confirm or deny it. The sign still just says repairs.

Nothing else on record puts a face to who runs it or what actually happens in the back. It's exactly the kind of storefront a city like this one is full of: functional, unremarkable, and worth a second look anyway.
