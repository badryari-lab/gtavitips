Snapmatic is [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city)'s social photo platform. as both a gameplay mechanic and an in-world cultural institution. **The Vice City influencer economy runs on Snapmatic.** The cartel money gets laundered through Snapmatic-adjacent businesses. The police use it to identify people.

The protagonist's Snapmatic account is functional in game. Photos taken in-world can be shared. The in-game feed shows what Vice City is doing and thinking and performing at any given moment. The platform is indistinguishable from its real-world inspiration except for the name and the fact that the content is occasionally more honest.

Characters in the game use Snapmatic the way real people use Instagram: to document the version of their life they want to present, to observe the version other people are presenting, and to make decisions based on the gap between both. **In Vice City that gap is measurably larger than in most places.**
