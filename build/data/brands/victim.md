Victim is the [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) clothing brand for the person who wants the garment to do some heavy lifting before they say a single word. The name is on the chest. The name is Victim. **Whether this is ironic, sincere, or aspirational is a question the brand leaves open by design.**

The product is well-made for the price. The construction is better than Binco. The aesthetic is intentionally confrontational without crossing into anything that would prevent it being sold in a mall environment. The line between those two things is where Victim operates.

In Vice City the brand appears alongside SubUrban and ZIP in the mall retail environment, which creates an interesting three-way proximity: the generic option, the aspirational option, and the option that wants the name on your chest to start a conversation before you have to. Victim is always the last one.
