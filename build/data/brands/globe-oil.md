Globe Oil is a big name on few forecourts. **Four stations in all: Watson Bay, [Hamlet](/gameplay/locations/hamlet), Crosstown in [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city), and Grayson up north.** Xero Gas runs far more pumps.

Every vehicle in Leonida burns fuel, and an empty tank leaves you on foot. Pull up beside a pump until Services appears: Refuel fills the tank and shows the price first, Repair patches the car up for a fee.

Gas stations appear as a blue pump once the fog clears. Vehicles, then Gas Stations, on the map lists every one you have found.
