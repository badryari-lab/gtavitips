Cool Beans arrived in [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) and occupied every viable corner retail space on the beachfront within a period that local commercial landlords describe as aggressive. **You are never more than one block from one.** This was the plan and it has been fully executed.

The menu is not a menu. It is a vocabulary test. There are base drinks, modifiers, sizes with proprietary names, milk alternatives with their own hierarchy, and a seasonal rotation that makes previous knowledge partially obsolete every few months. The barista will spell your name incorrectly. This is consistent across all locations.

The actual function of a Cool Beans in Vice City is to provide temperature-controlled public space without requiring social interaction. Laptop open, overpriced beverage, two hours of ambient music that nobody selected. **The coffee is how you pay for the chair.** This model has been extraordinarily successful.
