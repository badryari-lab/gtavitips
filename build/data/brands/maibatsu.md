Maibatsu is not a glamorous brand. It is a functional one. The Mule is in every commercial district, every industrial zone, and parked outside every warehouse in Leonida. It is also the vehicle most frequently appropriated by the protagonist for reasons that were not on the delivery manifest.

The Sanchez is the dirt bike. Two variants confirmed in Leonida: the standard Sanchez and the Livery edition. Both are built for the kind of terrain that appears between the end of the road and the destination. **The Penumbra is the civilian saloon, a competent mid-range vehicle that disappears into Leonida traffic with no difficulty.**

Maibatsu does not have a flagship sports car. It does not need one. The Mule alone has more screen time across the GTA series than most manufacturer's entire catalogue.
