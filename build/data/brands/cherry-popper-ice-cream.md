Cherry Popper Ice Cream has been in [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) across multiple GTA titles. The trucks are pink. The jingle carries for three blocks in any direction and has been the subject of noise complaints in four Vice City residential districts. The complaints have not resulted in route changes.

The ice cream is sold. Soft-serve, seasonal novelties, the standard cone at a price that has remained stable while every other food item in Vice City has increased. **The pricing stability is not a gesture of goodwill. It is a function of the secondary revenue stream that the primary business supports.**

VCPD has reviewed the Cherry Popper operation at various points in the franchise's Leonida history. Each review has concluded without actionable findings. The trucks maintain their routes. The jingle plays on schedule. The business continues at the volume that Vice City generates for it.
