Horny's has been on the [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) beachfront since before most of the current buildings around it were constructed. **The wings are genuinely good.** The beer is cold. The rest of the public discourse about the establishment is separate from the food, which continues to be the reason a portion of the clientele actually goes there.

The menu covers wings in eight sauce configurations, a beer list that reads the room correctly, and a series of other items that exist to make the wing order feel like a full meal rather than a decision made in the car park. The franchise has never apologized for any element of the brand and does not intend to.

The Vice City strip location and the suburban stadium-adjacent location serve different demographics at different hours and occasionally the same demographic at the same hours but in different clothes. Horny's does not distinguish between them operationally. The food is the same. The price is the same. The experience is calibrated to the customer who shows up.
