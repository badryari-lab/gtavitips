Karin has the longest track record of any manufacturer in the GTA universe. The Sultan has been in every major entry in the series. In GTA 6 it returns in two configurations: the standard Sultan for everyday use and the Sultan RS for the street racing circuit.

The Futo is the drift specialist. Rear-wheel drive, lightweight, and the preferred vehicle of anyone who learned to drive on a mountain road at three in the morning. **The Rebel is the off-road counterpart: a truck body over a performance chassis for the interior of Leonida.**

Four confirmed models. No other Japanese manufacturer in Leonida has this range. Karin is not niche. It is the backbone of the performance vehicle market across the entire state.
