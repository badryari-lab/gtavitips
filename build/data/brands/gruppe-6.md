Gruppe 6 moves cash. Armored trucks, scheduled routes, the kind of statewide infrastructure that keeps banks and businesses stocked without anyone having to think about how the money physically gets from one building to another. It's not a brand anyone notices until a truck is sitting at the curb, back doors open, guards standing around doing nothing interesting.

**That's exactly the moment it becomes interesting.** One of Gruppe 6's trucks is hit during a stretch of crime running across [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city), alongside a run of convenience store robberies. Armored trucks carrying cash on a fixed schedule are, by design, an easier target than a bank vault. Gruppe 6's whole business model runs directly through that risk.

The company itself doesn't get a face in the footage. No named executives, no headquarters shown. It's infrastructure, the kind of institution that's everywhere in the background of Leonida's economy and never the subject of its own story, until a crew decides otherwise.
