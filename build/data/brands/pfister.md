Pfister is the German manufacturer in Leonida's performance vehicle market. The brand sounds like it should be pronounced differently in English. The brand has known this for the entire history of its presence in Leonida and has never changed it. This is either confidence or indifference and the outcome is the same.

The performance credentials are genuine. The Pfister is the car for the [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) driver who read the specification sheet before making the purchase. The handling data, the weight distribution, the braking numbers. **The Pfister buyer did the work before arriving at the dealership and the car justifies the preparation.**

In Vice City the Pfister sits between Ocelot and Grotti. More precise than the Ocelot. Less theatrical than the Grotti. The choice of the person who wants to win the corner, not the conversation about the corner.
