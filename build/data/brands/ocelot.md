Ocelot is the British manufacturer in Leonida's luxury vehicle market. The cars are visually restrained in a way that communicates confidence rather than modesty. The person who buys an Ocelot in [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) is not trying to be seen. They are trying to drive.

The performance numbers are not modest. The restraint is entirely aesthetic. Under the exterior the engineering is committed in ways that become apparent at speed on the Vice City coast road at the hour when the road is clear enough to find out. **The Ocelot is what happens when a country that is very good at engineering decides to be very quiet about it.**

In gameplay terms, the Ocelot occupies the high-handling, mid-exotic range. Fast enough to be a mission vehicle, composed enough to be controlled at that speed. The choice for players who want performance without the Grotti price tag or the Pegassi visual statement.
