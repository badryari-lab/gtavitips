The standard Sultan is a good car. The Sultan RS is what happens when someone takes a good car and removes everything that made it comfortable, replaces it with a roll cage and a stiffer suspension, then turns up the turbo. It is not a subtle upgrade. The wide-body kit announces itself. The exhaust announces itself louder.

In Leonida, the RS sits in the space between serious track tool and [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) street flex. **The AWD system gives it traction that most rear-wheel cars cannot match out of a corner**, and on the wet asphalt around the port or the loose gravel of the rural routes, that advantage becomes decisive. It is not the fastest car in a straight line. It is the car that takes corners faster than the car behind it and exits them already accelerating.

The turbo flutter on lift-off is audible. The anti-lag crackle on downshift is louder. On tight parking deck sequences you can hear where it is before you can see it. **That is either useful intelligence or a significant tactical liability**, depending on which side of a pursuit you are on.
