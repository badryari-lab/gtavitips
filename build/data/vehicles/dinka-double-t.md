The Double-T is the Dinka sport motorcycle. Japanese full-fairing, aggressive forward lean geometry, and the engine output of a litre-class sport bike. In [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) traffic this translates to the ability to use road space that no car can access.

The sport motorcycle lane-splits, filter through stopped traffic, and takes the line through a corner that no four-wheel vehicle can follow. **In Vice City's tourist corridor, the Double-T is faster than any car at any time the traffic is above light density.**

The handling demands the same awareness that all sport bikes require: the performance is available faster than the consequences of misusing it. In the city grid, the Double-T is the fastest vehicle from light to light. On the highway, it is the fastest vehicle until something with four wheels and forced induction makes a different argument.
