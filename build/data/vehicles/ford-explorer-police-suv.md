The Explorer Police SUV is the AWD law enforcement utility vehicle across Leonida. The real-world Ford Explorer Police Interceptor is one of the most common law enforcement vehicles in Florida, which makes its presence in Leonida's police fleet the correct call.

The AWD platform gives it capability across the range of Leonida's environments: the [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) urban grid, the [Vice County](/gameplay/locations/vice-county) suburban roads, and the rural territory where front-wheel drive patrol cars lose their capability advantage.

**The Explorer Police SUV appears in multiple agency configurations across Leonida.** The [VCPD](/gameplay/gangs-and-factions/vcpd), [VCSO](/gameplay/gangs-and-factions/vcso), and [State Police](/gameplay/gangs-and-factions/leonida-state-police) all use the platform in configurations appropriate to their jurisdiction.
