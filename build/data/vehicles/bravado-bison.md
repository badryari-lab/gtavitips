The Bison is the full-size Bravado pickup. In [Vice County](/gameplay/locations/vice-county) this is the default vehicle class: the truck that the agricultural and construction population drives because it does everything a working truck needs to do. **Heavy duty, available everywhere, and capable enough for the specific demands of Vice County terrain.**

The Ram profile translates directly. The full-size pickup is the dominant vehicle class in rural America and in Leonida's county equivalent. The Bison is not a performance vehicle. It is the vehicle that the performance vehicles occasionally need pulling out of a ditch.

In [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) the Bison is the vehicle of the contractor, the person delivering something, and the Vice County resident who drove into the city for a specific reason and will return to the county when that reason is addressed.
