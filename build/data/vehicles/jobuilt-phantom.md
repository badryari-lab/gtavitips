The Phantom is the full-size American semi-truck. The Peterbilt long-nose profile: chrome exhaust stacks, long hood, and the specific presence of an object that is too large and too heavy to treat as a traffic participant the same way everything smaller is treated.

On Leonida's highway network the Phantom moves cargo between [Port Gellhorn](/gameplay/locations/port-gellhorn), [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city), and the state interior. The logistics infrastructure the Mule services in the city, the Phantom services on the highway.

**As a mission vehicle the Phantom is the mission vehicle.** Steal the cab, attach the trailer, move the cargo, manage the pursuit with forty tonnes of momentum. The mass of the vehicle is the asset and the liability simultaneously.
