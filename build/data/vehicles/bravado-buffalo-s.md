The Buffalo S is what happens when someone adds the performance package to the already capable civilian Buffalo. More power, stiffer suspension, and the visual upgrades that communicate the difference to people who know what to look for. **To everyone else it looks like a Buffalo that has been cleaned recently.**

The performance sedan category is the most practical tier in the [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) vehicle hierarchy. Fast enough for every situation Leonida creates, unassuming enough that the response it generates is proportional to the situation rather than to the vehicle's presence.

In Vice City, the Buffalo S is the car that surprises people. The civilian profile until the moment the performance calibration becomes relevant.
