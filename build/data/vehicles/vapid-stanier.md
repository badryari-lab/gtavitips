The Stanier has been moving through Leonida in one form or another since before most of the people driving it were born. The '55 variant is the starting point. Two-tone body panels, chrome front end, and proportions that belong to the era when American car design was more interested in presence than aerodynamics.

Shore Court comes with it. **Personal garage, weapon locker, and stolen goods storage.** Everything needed to set up in [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) without sourcing it separately. The Stanier is the pre-order bonus for both editions.

The trunk question is a Leonida tradition. [Vapid](/gameplay/brands/vapid) built it big in 1955 and nobody since has seen a reason to ask what it was for.

How to get it: pre-order the game, Standard or Ultimate, and reach Chapter 3. A task shows up under Exploration in the map index. Drive to the [Ocean View Hotel](/gameplay/locations/ocean-view-hotel) in [Ocean Beach](/gameplay/locations/ocean-beach) and take the car from the curb. **Collect it first: the rest of the pack stays locked until you do.** After that the outfits wait in your wardrobe, the hairstyles sit in the Special section of any salon, and the palm-print weapon pattern is under Cosmetics / Wraps at any Ammu-Nation.
