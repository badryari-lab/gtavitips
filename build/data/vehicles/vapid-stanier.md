The Stanier has been moving through Leonida in one form or another since before most of the people driving it were born. The '55 variant is the starting point. Two-tone body panels, chrome front end, and proportions that belong to the era when American car design was more interested in presence than aerodynamics.

Shore Court comes with it. **Personal garage, weapon locker, and stolen goods storage.** Everything needed to set up in [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) without sourcing it separately. The Stanier is the pre-order bonus for all editions, which makes it the first vehicle confirmed for every player who locked in before launch.

The trunk question is a Leonida tradition. [Vapid](/gameplay/brands/vapid) built it big in 1955 and nobody since has seen a reason to ask what it was for.
