The Rebel is the Karin off-road vehicle. The FJ Cruiser profile: boxy, distinctive, and built entirely for what it does away from the road. In [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) the Rebel is unusual. In [Vice County](/gameplay/locations/vice-county) it is the correct vehicle for the territory.

The Florida interior terrain that the [Grassrivers](/gameplay/locations/grassrivers) and the rural Vice County roads represent is the Rebel's natural environment. High clearance, four-wheel drive, and the suspension articulation that loose surface driving requires.

**The Rebel is the vehicle that connects the Vice City road network to the Vice County terrain that roads do not service.** Trail access, agricultural land margins, and the areas where the road transitions to track are all Rebel territory.
