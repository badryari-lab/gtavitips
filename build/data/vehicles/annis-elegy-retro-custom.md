The Elegy Retro Custom is the Skyline GT-R that the [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) tuner scene built. The R32 profile is the starting point. Everything beyond the profile is the product of Vice City's import car culture: wide body kit, period-correct but performance-upgraded mechanicals, and the AWD turbocharged system that made the original Skyline the benchmark for its generation.

The tuner culture context is the correct frame for the Elegy Retro Custom. This is not a car you buy from a dealership. It is a car you build, or you find someone who built it correctly, and the cost reflects both the parts and the knowledge. **In Vice City's car meets the Elegy Retro Custom earns a specific kind of respect that a factory hypercar cannot buy.**

The AWD system makes the turbocharged power accessible from traction-limited situations. The wide body accommodates the track width that the suspension geometry requires. The combination produces a car that is fast, controllable, and unmistakeable.
