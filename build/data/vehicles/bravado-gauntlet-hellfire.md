The Gauntlet Hellfire is Bravado's take on the Challenger SRT Hellcat. Wide body, supercharger bulge in the hood, rear-wheel drive, and **more power than the back tyres can always put down.**

No dealer sells you one. It is a target on **Randy's Wish List**, the board of rare cars Randy Kozlov wants delivered to Leonida Auto World in Vice City. The board opens in Chapter 3.

This one sits at the back of a junkyard in [Hamlet](/gameplay/locations/hamlet), just east of Boone Speedway. Copy its key with the Key Cloner on your phone and drive it out the gate.

Most cars on the board carry a tracker, so keep moving until it is in Randy's garage. He pays for condition: a clean delivery earns a $10,000 bonus, so fix any damage at a gas station on the way in.
