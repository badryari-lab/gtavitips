The Gauntlet Hellfire is the muscle car. The supercharged V8, the rear-wheel drive, and the wide body that communicates what is under the hood before the engine starts. The Challenger SRT is the real-world reference and the Hellfire captures the specific character of the Hellcat variant: more power than the rear wheels can always use, delivered with a supercharger whine that Vice City hears from the next block.

The muscle car formula is the opposite of the European sports car approach. **Maximum power, minimum aerodynamic complexity, rear-wheel drive, and the handling that follows.** The Gauntlet Hellfire makes the formula work by committing to it entirely.

The [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) strip is the Gauntlet's natural environment. Long straights, traffic to navigate around, and the specific audience that the muscle car generation performs for.
