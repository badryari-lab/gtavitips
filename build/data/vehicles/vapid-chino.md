The Chino is the Vapid lowrider. Same classic American platform as the Declasse Tornado, different brand context. The lowrider scene in [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) has enough depth to support multiple classic platforms and the Chino occupies its position within it.

**The Chino at base specification is a classic full-size American car in reasonable condition.** The Chino at full custom specification is months of work expressed in hydraulics, paint, and chrome. Both exist in Vice City simultaneously.

The lowrider culture in real Miami is part of the city's cultural DNA in the same way the import tuner scene is. The Chino represents the classic American side of Vice City's car culture alongside the Tornado.
