The Sanchez Livery is the competition-decorated version. The mechanical specification matches the standard Sanchez. The livery graphics package is the distinction: the sponsor colours, the competition numbers, and the visual language of a motorcycle that is supposed to be on a track or a trail rather than a [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) side street.

In Vice City's car and motorcycle culture the livery version carries the credibility of something associated with actual competition rather than the street. **It is also more visible, which in Vice City is either an advantage or a problem depending on the situation.**

The Sanchez Livery appears in the off-road competition context: race events, the [Vice County](/gameplay/locations/vice-county) trail scene, and the specific situations where competition aesthetics are the correct visual statement.
