The Comet 2 is the water-cooled transition generation of the Pfister Comet. The air-cooled era ended and the Comet 2 is what came next: similar profile, updated mechanicals, and the handling character of a 911 that has been refined rather than redesigned.

In Leonida's vehicle landscape, having multiple Comet variants means the choice the specific expression of the Pfister rear-engine philosophy. The Retro Custom is the heritage piece. The S2 is the current-generation sports car. **The Comet 2 sits in the middle: accessible to a larger budget, still a Pfister, still rear-engine.**

The performance is honest. Not the fastest car available but faster than most of what it encounters in [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) traffic, and with handling that rewards the driver who understands what a rear-engine layout does at the limit.
