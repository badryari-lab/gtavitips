The Ruiner carries the Firebird's legacy directly. The Trans Am generation of the Firebird is what the original GTA Vice City was set against: the car of Miami Vice, the car of the era, the car that the [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) of the 1980s put on its poster. Returning it to present-day Leonida gives it the patina of something that survived everything the city went through.

The V8 and the rear-wheel drive are the classic muscle formula. Not the fastest car in a straight line against the modern performance roster. Not the most sophisticated through the technical sections. **The most characterful V8 soundtrack in the Leonida vehicle population and the car that Vice City recognizes the moment the engine starts.**

The Ruiner's place in Leonida is both practical and narrative. It works as a daily driver in Vice City, and it reads to series veterans as a marker of exactly where this lineage runs deepest.
