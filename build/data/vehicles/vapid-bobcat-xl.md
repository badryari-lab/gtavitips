The Bobcat XL is the F-Series of Leonida. The best-selling vehicle platform in the American market has its GTA equivalent here, and the reasons for its dominance in real life translate directly to Leonida: it does everything a working vehicle needs to do at a price and with a dealer network that makes alternatives harder to justify.

In [Vice County](/gameplay/locations/vice-county) the Bobcat XL is the default vehicle in the same way the Declasse sedan is the default in [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city). **Not the most interesting choice. The correct choice for the people who need a full-size pickup in the Vice County context.**

The towing capacity, the cargo bed, and the four-wheel drive option make the Bobcat XL the vehicle for Vice County's agricultural and construction sector. The person with a Bobcat XL in Vice County has it because it is what the job requires.
