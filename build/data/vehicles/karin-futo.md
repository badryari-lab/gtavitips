The Futo is the AE86 of Leonida. The car that anime made famous, that real-world time attack and drift culture built a mythology around, and that the [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) car scene has been modifying, racing, and arguing about since the first one arrived in the state.

The specification is modest by Vice City standards. Naturally aspirated engine, rear-wheel drive, lightweight body. No turbocharged power delivery, no all-wheel drive traction management. **The Futo is the car where the driver's skill is the performance variable.** A skilled driver in a Futo is faster through the technical sections than most drivers in faster cars.

The drift culture connection is direct. The AE86's rear-wheel drive and weight distribution make it the classic drift car configuration. In Vice City's street takeovers and drift events, the Futo earns the specific respect that a car earns when it is used correctly for what it was built for.
