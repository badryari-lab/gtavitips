The Ganado is what [Jason](/gameplay/characters/jason-duval) has to work with. A 1970 [Vapid](/gameplay/brands/vapid) with a car's nose and a pickup's bed, low to the road, faded and dented. Rockstar calls it well-worn. That is generous.

Ultimate Edition owners get the fix. **The Ganado Retro Build is a full kit that brings back the muscle and the classic styling:** yellow paint, red stripes over the hood and down the flanks, a scoop stamped 434ci, chrome slot wheels, the bull badge in a mesh grille. Same truck underneath. Nobody would guess.

The kit lives at Coastal Auto Care in [Key Lento](/gameplay/locations/key-lento). Roll in, pick Full Kits, and it costs nothing. The Retro Build fits Jason's Ganado and nothing else, so it is not a look you can put on another car.

On a coast road with the water on one side, it stops looking like a hand-me-down. It looks like the car Jason meant to have.

Timing matters: the Retro Build is a Chapter 1 bonus only. **Fit it to Jason's Ganado before the chapter ends**, because chapter-bound edition items do not come back once you move on.
