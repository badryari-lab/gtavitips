The Squalo is the boat for people who say they are going fishing. A cabin under the foredeck, a swept arch over the helm, teak on the swim platform and a cooler that fits a day out. Rods ride along. So does a weapons crate packed with explosives, which changes what a day out can mean.

It comes docked at [Washington Beach](/gameplay/locations/washington-beach), painted in a gradient that runs from pink at the bow to blue at the stern, palm leaves printed over the lot. Nobody mistakes it for a charter boat. Rockstar points it at Gambit Bay, for casting a line and [reeling in](/gameplay/activities/fishing) whatever bites.

Rockstar calls it open-ocean-ready, and the crate is the reason. **Out past the channel markers the Squalo stops being a family cruiser and starts being a floating armory.**

Shitzu builds the working boats on this coast, the [Longfin](/gameplay/vehicles/shitzu-longfin) among them. The Squalo is the one it built for the weekend, then armed anyway.

How to get it: own the Ultimate Edition and reach Chapter 4. The game tells you when it is ready, with a pop-up or a collection task in the menu.
