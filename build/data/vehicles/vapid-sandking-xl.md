The Sandking XL is the full-size off-road truck. The F-350 Super Duty in the lifted off-road configuration: body lift, suspension lift, off-road tyres, and the mechanical specification that makes the extra expense over a standard pickup justified for the terrain.

In [Vice County](/gameplay/locations/vice-county) the Sandking XL is the vehicle that goes where the Bobcat XL stops. The additional clearance and the heavier off-road specification open the [Vice County](/gameplay/locations/vice-county) backcountry, the [Grassrivers](/gameplay/locations/grassrivers) margins, and the agricultural access roads that the standard pickup treats as the edge of its range.

**The Sandking XL is the off-road capability ceiling in the standard truck category.** Below the Liberator monster truck and above the standard pickups.
