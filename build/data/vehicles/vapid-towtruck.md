The Towtruck is the vehicle recovery tool. It removes parked vehicles, abandoned vehicles, and vehicles that have been involved in incidents from the [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) street network and deposits them somewhere the owner will have difficulty recovering them from without paying a fee.

In Leonida the Towtruck has been used for vehicle theft, vehicle delivery, and the specific mechanics that the hook-and-chain or wheel-lift system enables. **A tow truck can move a vehicle without the driver's cooperation, which is exactly what certain missions require.**

The Vice City context adds the Towtruck to the commercial vehicle traffic: the parking enforcement circuit on the tourist strip, the recovery operations after incidents on the highway, and the standard commercial presence that makes it unremarkable on most Vice City roads.
