The Dominator Buggy starts as a '67 [Vapid](/gameplay/brands/vapid) fastback and ends somewhere nobody at the factory planned for. The body sits high on a lifted chassis, the wheels wear lug tires off a tractor, and a pipe bull bar and skid plate cover the nose. One headlight is taped over. Four lamps ride a bar on the roof. The spare hangs off the back.

Rockstar calls it a Mud Club monster, and the paint backs that up: navy with yellow stripes, a race number on the doors, sponsor decals on the fenders. This is a car that goes [mudding](/gameplay/activities/mudding) on weekends and does not get washed on Monday. **The backwoods of [Mount Kalaga](/gameplay/locations/mount-kalaga-national-park) are where it earns its keep.**

It does not come alone. The Paradise Garage in Watson Bay is part of the deal, with a weapon locker and a secure spot to drop stolen goods for the fence. A place to park the Buggy, and a place to keep what it hauls back.

Inside, it is still a sixties pony car. Three-spoke wheel, round gauges, mud on the glass.

How to get it: own the Ultimate Edition and reach Chapter 2. Collect the Buggy and the Classic Car Collection opens after it. The game tells you when it is ready, with a pop-up or a collection task in the menu.
