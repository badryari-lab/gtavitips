The Sanchez is the dirt bike in Leonida. The [Vice County](/gameplay/locations/vice-county) rural interior, the [Grassrivers](/gameplay/locations/grassrivers) margins, and the trails of the [Red Hill Forest](/gameplay/locations/red-hill-forest) are all Sanchez territory. **Light weight, high suspension travel, and the knobby tyre profile that loose surface riding requires.**

The Sanchez has been on Vice City streets since before most of the current buildings were there. It is the off-road motorcycle that reaches the locations the road network does not service. The dirt bike is not fast on road. On the Vice County trails it is faster than anything else available.

The handling reflects the dirt bike character. Responsive to weight shifts, willing to wheelie under power, and capable of the surface changes that Vice County terrain presents in sequence.
