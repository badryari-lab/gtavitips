The Carbon RS is the performance sport bike. The carbon fibre construction, the high-output engine, and the RS designation mark this as the maximum specification in the Nagasaki sport bike range. The Kawasaki H2 is the real-world benchmark for factory supercharged sport bikes, and the Carbon RS captures that specification.

The performance is the highest available in the two-wheel category. **Faster in a straight line than anything else in the Leonida motorcycle roster.** The carbon construction reduces weight enough to affect the handling noticeably.

The [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) environment does not allow most of the Carbon RS's potential to be used. The road grid is too short between intersections and too populated to sustain the speed the bike is capable of for long. The highway network and the coastal road are the environments where the Carbon RS is the correct choice.
