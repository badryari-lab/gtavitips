The Dodo is one of the more unlikely vehicles to survive from early Vice City aviation lore into the present. The seaplane configuration that made it a legend in its first appearance becomes genuinely useful in Leonida's Keys geography rather than merely unusual.

Water take-off and landing from any calm surface. The outer Keys islands, the Lake Leonida shoreline, the [Grassrivers](/gameplay/locations/grassrivers) channels wide enough for the wingspan. **The Dodo connects Leonida's water geography to its air geography in a single vehicle.**

The handling reflects the seaplane configuration. Water operations require open water approach and take-off runs. On land, the amphibious undercarriage allows conventional runway use. The Dodo is not the fastest or the most practical aircraft in the Leonida roster. It is the most versatile.
