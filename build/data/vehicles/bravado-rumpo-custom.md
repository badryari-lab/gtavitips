The Rumpo Custom is the van that [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city)'s less legitimate operations use when they need to move things that a sedan cannot carry and that do not benefit from being visible. The custom exterior modifications mean it does not look like a standard delivery vehicle, which is either a disadvantage or exactly the point depending on the operation.

The cargo capacity is the primary practical feature. **What the Rumpo Custom carries is determined by who is driving it.** The vehicle itself is neutral on the subject.

In Vice City, the Rumpo Custom appears in specific crew and faction contexts. The modification aesthetic places it in the territory between the custom car culture and the working vehicle category.
