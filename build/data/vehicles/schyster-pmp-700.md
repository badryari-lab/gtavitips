The PMP 700 is the American executive sedan alternative. The Chrysler 300 formula: long hood, formal upright body, rear-wheel drive, and the V8 option that makes the American executive car distinctly American. In [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) this formula sells to a specific demographic that the German brands do not reach.

The 300 generation Chrysler was a genuine cultural moment in real Miami: the car that appeared in the hip-hop video economy at the same time it appeared in the rental fleet. **The PMP 700 captures this dual-register presence.**

As a Vice City vehicle the PMP 700 appears in both the legitimate business district and the less legitimate economy that Vice City's mid-level operators use as their daily driver. The car is common enough to be unremarkable and nice enough to communicate that the owner is doing adequately.
