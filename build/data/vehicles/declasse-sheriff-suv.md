The Sheriff SUV is the VCSO's standard patrol vehicle. The Tahoe platform: four-wheel drive for the [Vice County](/gameplay/locations/vice-county) roads, the SUV capacity for the equipment and the occasional prisoner transport, and the law enforcement package that marks it as a county sheriff vehicle.

In Vice County, the Sheriff SUV is the equivalent of the VCPD's Police Buffalo: the ambient law enforcement presence on the roads outside the city limits. The response time is longer than the VCPD's but the vehicle is present throughout the county network.

**The four-wheel drive gives the Sheriff SUV capability in the Vice County terrain that the rear-wheel drive police cars lack.** Wet fields, unpaved access roads, and the rural conditions that call the VCSO out at night are all within the vehicle's capability.
