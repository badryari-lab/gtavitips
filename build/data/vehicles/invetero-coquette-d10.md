The Coquette D10 is Invetero's front-engine V8 sports car, rear-wheel drive, built on the Corvette formula. In a [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) full of Italian exotics, **it makes the American case at full throttle.**

It is a target on **Randy's Wish List**, the board of rare cars Randy Kozlov wants delivered to Leonida Auto World in Vice City. The board opens in Chapter 3.

The yellow one sits in a storage yard on the west docks of Waxman Island, behind a blue fence. Go over the fence on the left side, find it next to a red shipping container, copy the key with the Key Cloner and drive. D10s also park at the big houses on the rich side of [Capri](/gameplay/locations/capri).

Most cars on the board carry a tracker, so keep moving until it is in Randy's garage. He pays for condition: a clean delivery earns a $10,000 bonus, so fix any damage at a gas station on the way in.
