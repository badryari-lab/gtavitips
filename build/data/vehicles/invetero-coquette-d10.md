The Coquette D10 is the Invetero performance offering. American sports car, rear-wheel drive, V8 engine. The formula is not new. The execution reflects six decades of American performance car development.

In a [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) market where the status vehicles are mostly Italian, German, or Japanese, the Coquette D10 is the domestic sports car making the case that the American formula remains valid. **It makes this argument with a V8 at full throttle, which is the most persuasive form of automotive argument available.**

The D10 suffix marks this as the specific variant in the Coquette family. The Coquette has appeared in multiple GTA games in various configurations. The D10 represents the current-generation Leonida version.
