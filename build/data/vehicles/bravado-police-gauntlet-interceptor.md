The Gauntlet Interceptor is the VCPD's muscle car. The Challenger platform in police specification: the supercharged V8, the wide body, and the law enforcement equipment that converts the civilian Hellfire into a police vehicle. **This is the VCPD vehicle that the [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) car culture recognizes at a distance.**

The Gauntlet Interceptor appears at the point in the wanted level escalation where the VCPD has determined the standard pursuit vehicles are not sufficient. The performance specification matches or exceeds most civilian vehicles in the Vice City roster.

The visual presence of the Gauntlet Interceptor in the mirror is distinct from the standard Police Buffalo. The width, the muscle car profile, and the specific police light configuration make it immediately identifiable.
