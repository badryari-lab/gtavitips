The Astron is the Pfister answer to the electric performance era. Porsche's Taycan is the real-world benchmark for electric performance cars, and the Leonida version brings that directly with the four-door fastback profile and the engineering approach that defines Pfister across the lineup.

The electric drivetrain removes the combustion soundtrack that has always been part of Pfister's performance identity. The Astron replaces it with the high-pitched motor whine at full load and the silence at everything below it. **In [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city), the Astron approaches intersections without announcement and departs them with a speed that the engine note did not predict.**

The engineering credentials translate to performance numbers that equal or exceed combustion competitors at the Pfister price point. The Astron makes the case that Pfister's commitment to engineering correctness applies to electric powertrains as directly as it does to combustion ones.
