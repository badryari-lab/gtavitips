The Blazer goes where the road stops. Small enough to navigate the Vice County terrain that road vehicles cannot access, capable enough on loose surface to reach the locations the motorcycles struggle with. **The quad bike is the accessible off-road option in Leonida.**

The Blazer appears throughout the [Vice County](/gameplay/locations/vice-county) landscape: at farms, at recreation areas, at the rural locations where the road ends and the ATV begins. The lifeguard and recreation configurations extend the platform's range into the beach environment and the Keys trail access.

The handling is the quad bike character: stable under normal conditions, willing to roll if the weight transfer is misjudged on the wrong terrain. The four-wheel contact patch and the low centre of gravity make the Blazer more stable than a motorcycle in the same conditions.
