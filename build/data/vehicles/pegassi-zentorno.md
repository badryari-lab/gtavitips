The Zentorno is confirmed back on [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) streets from Trailer 1. The car that defined the previous generation of Leonida hypercar ownership is here. The car that everyone chased and everyone wanted to steal comes back for Leonida. **The profile matches: the hexagonal exhausts, the active aerodynamic elements, the extreme geometric bodywork that makes other cars look approximate by comparison.**

In Vice City the Zentorno belongs to the cartel money, the influencer money, and the people who have made enough of something to put it in a car that announces it from three blocks away. The sound at full throttle is not subtle. The design is not subtle. The Zentorno has never attempted subtlety and has never needed to.

The AWD system makes the power manageable in Vice City's street grid. The turns are tighter than a race track and the pedestrian traffic is less predictable. The all-wheel traction means the power goes to the road rather than producing wheelspin at every corner exit. The Zentorno uses this advantage over rear-wheel exotics in exactly the situations Vice City creates.
