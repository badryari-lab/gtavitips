The Baller is the luxury SUV in [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city). The Range Rover association is deliberate: the full-size profile, the elevated ride height, and the price that communicates the owner's financial position without requiring a sports car to do it.

In Vice City the Baller serves the demographic that needs SUV capacity alongside status vehicle presence. The person picking up from the airport in a Baller is making a different statement from the person picking up in a Jubilee. **The Baller says comfortable money. The Jubilee says generational money.**

The off-road capability is present but largely irrelevant in Vice City. In [Vice County](/gameplay/locations/vice-county) it becomes relevant. The Baller in the [Keys](/gameplay/locations/leonida-keys) is the vacation vehicle. The Baller in the [Port Gellhorn](/gameplay/locations/port-gellhorn) corridor is the logistics management vehicle. Both uses are present in Leonida.
