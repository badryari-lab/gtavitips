The Omnis E-GT is Leonida's engagement with the electric performance vehicle market. The real-world RS E-Tron GT is among the most capable electric performance cars available, and the Leonida version translates that directly: four doors, all-wheel drive, and the specific acceleration characteristic of electric motors.

The [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) driving environment makes the instant torque relevant in ways a combustion car cannot match. From a standing start, the E-GT applies all of its power immediately. No gear change, no torque curve to manage, no lag between input and output. **In the stop-start traffic of Vice City's tourist corridor this characteristic is not relevant. On the approach to the highway it is decisive.**

The silence at low speed is the E-GT's most unusual characteristic in a game that has always used engine sound as both entertainment and tactical information. The Omnis E-GT approaches at near-zero noise until the motor load increases. The Vice City pedestrian population has not adjusted to this yet.
