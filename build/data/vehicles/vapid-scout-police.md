The Scout Police is the compact police SUV. Smaller than the Explorer and the Tahoe in the police fleet, and more maneuverable in the [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) grid and the beach access routes where the larger vehicles have clearance limitations.

The Vice Beach Police application is the most visible use: the compact SUV navigating the tourist corridor and beach access points where the standard patrol car and the full-size SUV are less practical. **The Scout Police is the vehicle that fits where the others do not.**

The AWD configuration gives it the capability the compact size would otherwise limit. On the beach margins and the [Vice County](/gameplay/locations/vice-county) suburban roads, the Scout Police handles the conditions adequately.
