Nothing about the Sentinel Classic Convertible is trying to impress anyone. The paint has faded past the point of caring, the lines are decades old, and the top comes down because that's the whole point of owning it. This is the opposite of the Ubermacht showroom cars: no performance badge, no wide fenders, just an old convertible that still runs.

It's [Jason's](/gameplay/characters/jason-duval) ride through [Vice City](/gameplay/locations/vice-city) when the day's business is finished. **The kind of car that says nothing about money and everything about someone who'd rather keep his head down.** [Lucia](/gameplay/characters/lucia-caminos) rides shotgun with the window down and a red bandana in her hair, which tells you more about the mood than the horsepower does.

The E30-era proportions put this squarely in the Sentinel Classic's lineage, the hardtop version of which has circulated Leonida for years. This is the convertible cut nobody had seen before.
