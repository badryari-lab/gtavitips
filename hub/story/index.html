Story & Missions

The GTA 6 story, chapter by chapter, with a page for every mission. Names are on show; what happens stays behind a click.

Light story spoilers on this page: chapter and mission names. Everything that happens sits behind a click, and nothing opens until you do.

The story runs through a prologue and five chapters, 94 missions in all, with control passing between Jason and Lucia. Each mission page covers the objective, rewards, anything you can miss and a full walkthrough video.

Prologue

Lucia, inside Hamlet Correctional, with a flashback to the outside.

1 mission page
Mission 1
Prologue
Lucia, inside. Talk your way through a counseling session and a robbery gone south.
Lucia · Hamlet Correctional
Spoiler: Prologue Tap to reveal

A cold open shows the night Lucia and Jason first meet, both trying to steal the same car. Two years on, Lucia is serving time and talking her way toward supervised release with her counselor, Stefanie.

The flashback shows how she got there: broke, she left her baby son Tomás at a café and robbed the payday loan store across the street. She was caught running back for him, and child services took the boy. Getting him back drives everything she does after this, and the release address she gives is Jason's.

Spoiler: Chapter 1 Tap to reveal

Jason runs errands and dirty work for Brian Heder around Key Lento with Cal: drops at sea, rent collections, a broker who stops answering, and a growing feud with a swamp crew called the Kingfishers. He already knows Lucia's boy is his when he picks her up from prison. She moves into his house on an ankle monitor and talks her way onto his work.

The jobs get bigger until a pickup at the Grassrivers visitor center turns into a police ambush. Jason is badly hurt, the house goes up in flames, and Lucia breaks him out of a Vice City hospital with the law and worse closing in.

By the end the pair are on the run with a bag a lot of people want back, and a police captain, Aldo Méndez, has pinned a dead officer on them.
