The Launch Trailer: Five Weeks Out

No warning, no countdown. Rockstar dropped ninety seconds of GTA VI on October 15, captured entirely on PS5 and scored by Justice. A bank job, a swamp, a plane, and Lucia's ankle tag, still on.

Confirmed October 25, 2026
October 15, 2026

What Rockstar Dropped

"Grand Theft Auto VI Launch Trailer" went up on Rockstar's YouTube channel, its X profile, and the official GTA VI site with no announcement beforehand. Rockstar's description says it was captured entirely in-game from a PlayStation 5. It runs about ninety seconds and ends on the date: November 19. Five weeks out.

Rockstar did not call it Trailer 3. Fans did, because the rumor mill spent months waiting for one, and then An Extended Look on Netflix took that slot in August. This is the last piece of marketing before the game is in hands. The full run is on the trailers page.

Source, Confirmed
Release date, platform capture, and soundtrack details come from Rockstar's own upload and description. Scene details below are cross-checked against launch-day coverage.
The sound

Justice Takes the Wheel

The trailer debuts "Flower in a Hurricane", a new single from the French duo Justice with Tame Impala, SZA, and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard. It is also on Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album, which can be pre-saved and pre-ordered on CD and vinyl.

Justice are not a guest spot. They are credited as the composers of the game's original score, and this is the first public proof of what that sounds like. Earlier trailers borrowed licensed tracks. This one belongs to the game. More on how Rockstar uses music in Music of Leonida.

On screen

A Job That Goes Wrong

Rockstar's framing is the story it has told since Trailer 2: an easy score goes wrong, and Jason and Lucia end up inside a criminal conspiracy that runs across Leonida. They only get out if they trust each other.

The footage backs it up. Bank heists. Police chases and car pursuits. A swamp. A sequence on a plane. Snakes. A shower scene between the two leads. Crowded streets and interiors, and people eating from buckets of chicken in the background, because Leonida does not stop for a robbery. Helicopters carrying a section of a bank vault into a building pick up a thread from the Extended Look.

Jason's military past gets a beat. So does the relationship, in the quieter moments between the action.

New detail

The Ankle Tag

Lucia wears an ankle monitor. It has been in Rockstar's key art and in Trailer 2 since the start, and it is back here. She is out of Leonida Correctional, but the state has not let go.

Analysis
A tag on a protagonist is a mechanic waiting to happen. Curfews, zones, a signal that follows her to every job. Rockstar did not say what it does in play. The trailer shows it and moves on, which is how they plant things they plan to use.
What's missing

What It Doesn't Say

Almost no pure gameplay. The trailer is cinematic storytelling with the action cut tight. No online mode. No new mechanics walked through, no map reveal, no price talk. Rockstar has not given a resolution, frame rate, or 60 fps mode, and nothing was said about PC. The only platforms named are PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

It also shows nothing the Extended Look had not already established. That is the point. Twenty-six minutes explained the game. Ninety seconds sells the feeling of it.

Frequently Asked

Frequently Asked

Is this Trailer 3?
Rockstar titled it "Grand Theft Auto VI Launch Trailer" and did not number it. Fans count it as the third trailer and the last one before release.
Did the release date change?
No. Grand Theft Auto VI launches November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The trailer's key art carries the date.
What song is in the launch trailer?
"Flower in a Hurricane" by Justice, featuring Tame Impala, SZA, and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard. It is also on Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album.
Is there a PC version?
Not announced. Rockstar has only named PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. A Q1 2027 PC date has been reported, but Rockstar has not confirmed it.
