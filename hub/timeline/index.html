GTA 6 Timeline

Every confirmed date, in order. From the February 2022 development confirmation to launch day. No fan theories, no invented windows. Just what Rockstar and Take-Two put on the record, and when.

Confirmed Updated August 6, 2026
February 4, 2022 Confirmed

Rockstar Confirms It's Coming

Rockstar folded the news into a community update about winding down Red Dead Online support. One line did the work: more resources were moving to "the next entry in the Grand Theft Auto series," already well underway.

September 18, 2022 Confirmed

The Leak

Ninety videos from an early build hit the internet overnight through a compromised Slack channel. Rockstar's response was short: extremely disappointed, work continues as planned. Three years later, Trailer 2 answered back directly. Jason on a rooftop, mid-sentence: "I'm just up here fixing some leaks." The full breakdown covers the line.

December 4, 2023 Confirmed

Trailer 1 Drops

Ninety seconds, Lucia in a prison jumpsuit, Vice City at golden hour. The first official look, released alongside a Fall 2025 release window. The full breakdown takes it apart frame by frame.

May 2, 2025 Confirmed

First Delay: Fall 2025 → May 2026

Take-Two pushed the date eight months out, from Fall 2025 to May 26, 2026. No footage, no fanfare. Just a new number on an earnings call.

May 6, 2025 Confirmed

Trailer 2: Jason and Lucia

Four days after the delay, Rockstar answered with three minutes of footage running mostly in-engine on a base PS5. Jason and Lucia together for the first time, Vice City in daylight. Everything we saw, shot by shot.

November 6, 2025 Confirmed

Second Delay: November 19, 2026 Locked

Six more months, this time explained: the extra time buys "the level of polish you have come to expect and deserve." The date has held since. Full details on the confirmation.

June 24–25, 2026 Confirmed

Pre-Orders and the PS5 Partnership

Standard and Ultimate editions go live at midnight. The same day, Sony and Rockstar put a name on what Trailer 2 already showed: this plays best on PS5. Pre-order details · the PS5 partnership.

August 6, 2026 Confirmed

Netflix Gets It First

Rockstar hands Netflix a six-hour head start on the next reveal, a first for the studio. Not a numbered trailer. Its own format, called An Extended Look. Full announcement.

August 27, 2026 Confirmed

An Extended Look

Premieres on Netflix at 3:00 PM ET, then goes public on YouTube six hours later. Everything confirmed and how to watch.

November 19, 2026 Confirmed

Launch Day

Leonida opens on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S. No PC date announced. Everything confirmed about the release.

Frequently Asked

Frequently Asked

When did Rockstar first confirm GTA 6 was in development?
February 4, 2022, in a community update about Red Dead Online's wind-down. Rockstar said resources were shifting to "the next entry in the Grand Theft Auto series."
How many times has GTA 6 been delayed?
Twice. Fall 2025 moved to May 26, 2026 on May 2, 2025, then May 26, 2026 moved to November 19, 2026 on November 6, 2025. The current date has held since.
When was GTA 6 leaked?
September 18, 2022. Ninety videos from an early build spread online through a compromised Slack channel. Rockstar confirmed development continued as planned.
What's the next confirmed date?
August 27, 2026: "An Extended Look," premiering on Netflix before going public on YouTube. Full details here.
