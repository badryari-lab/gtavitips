GTA 6 Trophy Guide

55 trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver and 46 bronze. On Xbox they add up to 1,000 Gamerscore. One playthrough covers all of it. No online trophies, no difficulty settings, and free roam keeps running after the credits, so whatever you skipped is still out there.

Plan on about 200 hours. Roughly a third of that is the story, most of the rest is the 100% checklist behind the gold A Long Road. That one is the grind. The rest come from living in Leonida: robbing, racing, hunting and scanning everything with WAiNK.

Almost nothing is missable. The one thing to watch: in Chapter 1, Lucia offers a meditation on the balcony at Jason's house. Take it. It disappears later, and nobody has pinned down yet whether the meditation trophy needs it. Keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter anyway.

Activities and Side Jobs

TrophyGradeHow to Get It
Cunning StuntsSilverFinish 250 of the 397 stunts. Every kind counts: stunt jumps, flying under bridges, knife flights and BASE jumps. Bridges go fastest.
Master of NoneBronzeTry every activity type once: fishing, hunting, golf, a shooting range, pool, basketball, an arcade or TV game, winning a race and a stunt. All activities.
The ShootistSilverGold medals in every challenge at the shooting ranges: 42 medals over 12 ranges. Your Focus levels up on the way.
Top FunBronzeWin a Top Fun drone race at all 14 locations. One win per spot is enough. Racing.
Earn Cash, Not KudosBronzeWin 20 different races out of the 40. Winning the same race twice counts once.
Baited My TackleBronzeBeat Cal at fishing. Some time after you meet him in Chapter 1, he texts you a spot. Go and win.
Stick FiddlerBronzeFinish one arcade game: Tenebrae Lux, Street Trash III or Time Jackers Rewind on the TV at home, or Bang For Your Buck on a cabinet.
Personal BestBronzeMax out Strength for Jason and Lucia. Take your partner to the gym: training one trains the other. The fitness system.
Fair GameBronzeHunt a unique animal for Bret. Jason only, from Chapter 2. Which animal does it is still unclear, but working through all 15 Target Decks covers it. Hunting.
Positive Mental AttitudeBronzeGet the meditation counter to 18. A spot done by both Jason and Lucia counts twice. Every meditation spot.
This Was No Boat AccidentBronzeFinish Kenton's last job. His diving work starts in Chapter 1: seven shipwrecks and a metal detector. He leaves for a while, then comes back.
Road to RehabilitationBronzeFinish all five jobs for Andy. Lucia only, from a call in Chapter 3. Still open after the story.
The Lone RiderBronzeFinish all five jobs for Billy at the Thrillbilly Mud Club. They open partway through Chapter 2.
Spin On This!BronzeFinish Tyrone's last job. Jason only, from Chapter 3: burn down the synthetic drug labs around Vice City first.
Strangers & FreaksSilverFinish 8 strands for strangers. Contacts with their own job chains (Moses, Tyrone, Yasmani, Andy, Billy) do not count. Watch the trophy tracker.

Collectibles and Exploration

TrophyGradeHow to Get It
Don't Touch My DeckSilverComplete any 3 of the 18 collectible card sets. Progress sits under Progress, Collections.
You WAiNKer!SilverScan 100 places with WAiNK: landmarks, curiosities, sculptures, No Flusher tags. WAiNK locations.
The Goodtime StateBronzeClear all the fog on the map. A plane over the dark patches does it fastest.
One Last ThingBronzeComplete 50 of the 80 challenges. Plenty finish on their own, and 100% wants 70 anyway. Leave it for late.
Actions Speak LouderBronzeMake 500 choices that move your Criminal Profile. It comes by itself. Stay away from the permanent Irredeemable profile.

Vehicles

TrophyGradeHow to Get It
Whip It GoodBronzeScan 100 vehicles with WAiNK. Stand by a busy road. You never have to drive them.
On the SpotBronzeRegister 10 vehicles in the Scooter Bros app. You need free garage space and every registration costs money, so cheap cars and bikes do the job.
Little BoxesBronzeBuy all 8 garages for sale, about $160,000 in total, in the Vice City Real Estate app. It arrives in Chapter 2. The garages the story gives you do not count.
Supply & DemandBronzeDeliver all 16 cars on Randy's wish list. It opens in Chapter 3 with the Key Cloner. How car theft works.
Challenging TankBronzeTake a Rhino from Mile Zero Air Station and kill with it. How to get a tank.
Catch Me If You CanBronzeSteal a passenger jet docked at a jet bridge at VCIA, not one out on the apron, and fly about 1 km away.
Capital OffenseBronzeHidden. Drive a car out of a vehicle showroom through the front window, the old-school way. Which showroom and which car count is not pinned down yet.
Ten PinBronzeKick 10 pedestrians off a bike within one minute. Busy Ocean Beach makes it easy.

Crime and Combat

TrophyGradeHow to Get It
Maximum WithdrawalSilverRob 5 of the 8 bank vaults and get away. They open through an optional Chapter 4 mission. Bring your partner for a second bag.
A Classic Blitz PlayBronzeRob an armored truck and escape. A bank vault job counts too, or stop a truck and blow its doors with a sticky bomb.
Retail TherapyBronzeRob 3 shops in a row with your partner without losing your Wanted Level. The three jewelry stores in north Vice City sit close together. The trophy takes about 30 seconds to pop.
SneakthiefBronzeRob a jewelry store unseen. Yasmani's jobs in Chapter 3 give you the Snap Gun: pick the back door, find the code note in the back room, empty the safe. Safe codes.
Pest ControlBronzeRob one Illegal Operation from each of the 9 gangs. 100% wants all 39. The gangs.
Let's Go BowlingBronzeAt Kingpin Bowling in Port Gellhorn, take the office watch, the Gutterball shotgun and the safe (code 0300), then lose the cops. Separate visits work.
Hot TogetherBronzeLose a 4-star Wanted Level with your partner along. Bank vaults always bring 4 stars, so it tends to happen anyway. The wanted system.
GunslingerBronzeDrop 5 enemies in one use of Combat Focus. Fill Focus first and bring an accurate assault rifle.
Dime on the DollarBronzeLaunder $250,000 in marked bills through your contacts. It adds up over time once Chapter 3 hands you the Dye Pack Jammer. Yasmani pays best.
Treat Yo SelfBronzeSpend $100,000 in shops, in total. Guns, car mods and tattoos burn it fastest. Making it back.

Related Questions
Are there missable trophies in GTA 6?
Not really. Free roam stays open after the credits and side jobs wait for you. The only doubt is the Chapter 1 balcony meditation with Lucia: take it, in case the meditation trophy needs it.
Source: GTA VI, in-game
How long does the GTA 6 platinum take?
About 200 hours in one playthrough. The story is the smaller part. Most of the time goes into the 100% checklist.
Source: GTA VI, in-game
Are there online trophies?
No. Every trophy is single player, and there are no difficulty settings to worry about.
Source: GTA VI, in-game
