The World

The state of Leonida. A fictional analog of modern Florida. Vice City is the main urban hub, based on Miami. The state covers a wide geographic range including the Leonida Keys, the Grassrivers wetlands, Port Gellhorn, and Mount Kalaga in the interior.

Source: Rockstar Trailer 1 and Trailer 2

About twice the size of GTA 5's map, says Rockstar's Aaron Garbut, and nearly as big as GTA IV, GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 combined. Rockstar has not given a figure in square miles.

Source: Aaron Garbut (Rockstar) to Game Informer and Edge

Yes, about twice as big. Rockstar's Aaron Garbut calls Leonida the biggest map the studio has built "by some degree": Vice City, the Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga on one map.

Source: Aaron Garbut (Rockstar) to Game Informer

No. The map is Leonida, from the Keys up to Lummox County on the northern edge. Gloriana only shows up on out-of-state license plates.

Six regions on one map: Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park. Every place with its own page sits under locations.
