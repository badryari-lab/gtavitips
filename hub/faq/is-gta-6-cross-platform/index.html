Is GTA 6 Cross-Platform?

Not confirmed. Rockstar has not addressed cross-platform play for GTA 6 Online. GTA Online has never been cross-platform across PlayStation and Xbox. PS5 players have always been in separate sessions from Xbox players.

The pattern across the entire GTA Online lifecycle strongly suggests GTA 6 Online will follow the same model. Cross-platform play would represent a significant departure from how Rockstar has operated every previous GTA Online release.

Related Questions
Will PS5 and Xbox players be in the same GTA 6 Online sessions? Analysis
Unlikely based on precedent. GTA Online across its entire lifecycle has kept PlayStation and Xbox players in separate sessions. Rockstar has never implemented cross-platform play. Without a specific announcement, the expected outcome is platform-separated sessions.
Analysis, based on GTA Online history
Is GTA 6 cross-generation? No
GTA 6 launches on PS5 and Xbox Series X/S only. There is no PS4 or Xbox One version. Cross-generation play is not applicable.
Source: Rockstar Newswire
Will GTA 6 have cross-progression between PS5 and Xbox? Analysis
Not confirmed and not expected based on precedent. GTA Online character progress has never been transferable between PlayStation and Xbox platforms. A cross-progression system would be a first for the series.
