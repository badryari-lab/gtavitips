How It Works

Every block in Leonida has a mart, and most forecourts have one bolted on. Grab a snack, top up, keep moving. Fast food gives a short boost to health, stamina or focus, some of it pushes you past your normal maximum for a while, and you can eat at the wheel. Convenience stores also stock Zombix, a heal you carry in your pocket, plus groceries for the fridge at home.

The fast food chains are their own crowd: Pollo Diablo, Marbles, Taco Bomb, Peewees and Chacey's, one Burger Shot on Ocean Beach and two waffle houses, Hank's Waffles and Frank's.

Every counter is also a till. Pull a weapon and the clerk empties the register. Bigger stores keep a safe in the back: get the four-digit code out of an employee or find where someone wrote it down, or blow it and live with the noise. The register pays pocket money. The safe holds the real cash and the jewelry a fence wants. Convenience stores show as a shopping bag and fast food as a burger once the fog clears, both under Map > Food & Drink.

Tips

  • Some food pushes your health past the maximum for a while. Eat before the fight.
  • Pick up Zombix at a convenience store. It heals, and it fits in a pocket.
  • No safe code? Lean on an employee or look around the back for where someone wrote it down. Blowing the safe is faster and a lot louder.
  • The register pays a couple of hundred dollars. The safe is where the jewelry is, and that goes to a fence.

Frequently Asked

Can you rob convenience stores in GTA 6?
Yes. Point a weapon at the clerk and they empty the register. If there is a safe, get the code from an employee or a note on the premises, or blow it open. The register only pays a couple of hundred dollars.
How do you heal in GTA 6?
Eat and drink. Fast food, street food and convenience store snacks restore health, some items overheal past the maximum, and Zombix from convenience stores heals too.
How many fast food restaurants are in GTA 6?
21 across eight names: Pollo Diablo (5), Marbles (4), Taco Bomb (4), Peewees (3), Chacey's (2), and one each of Burger Shot, Frank's Waffles and Hank's Waffles.
Is there a Burger Shot in GTA 6?
Yes, one you can eat at, on Ocean Beach.
How many convenience stores are in GTA 6?
72, listed under Map > Food & Drink > Convenience Stores. Most of them are in Vice-Dale County.
