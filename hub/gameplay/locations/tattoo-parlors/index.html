How It Works

Ink is a commitment, so pick the studio before the design. Every parlor in Leonida stocks its own style. Anime at one, Old School flash at another, stick-and-poke out by Port Gellhorn. If you want a look, you go where it hangs on the wall.

Jason and Lucia can both sit in the chair. Walk in, browse the designs and pay for what you like. Most of the studios crowd into Vice City and Vice Beach, two work out of Kelly County, and the Keys have one, on Key Lento.

Studios show as an orange heart once the fog clears. Map > Style > Tattoos lists every one and keeps count of the ones you have found. Electric Fang Tattoo, the Stockyard pop-up, only opens its doors to Ultimate Edition owners.

Tips

  • Shop by style, not by distance. The design you want only hangs on one studio's wall.
  • Both Jason and Lucia can get work done, so the same studio serves either of them.
  • Out in the Keys there is exactly one option: The Inked Mermaid on Key Lento.

Frequently Asked

How many tattoo shops are in GTA 6?
Eleven. Eight in Vice-Dale County, two in Kelly County and one in Mariana County, The Inked Mermaid on Key Lento. Electric Fang Tattoo is one of the eleven and needs the Ultimate Edition.
Can Lucia get tattoos in GTA 6?
Yes. Jason and Lucia can both buy tattoos at any studio.
How do you unlock Electric Fang Tattoo in GTA 6?
Own the Ultimate Edition. The pop-up studio in Stockyard opens in Chapter 1 and shows under Map > Style > Tattoos.
How do you find tattoo shops in GTA 6?
Clear the fog and look for the orange heart icon, or open Map > Style > Tattoos for the full list and a counter of the ones you have found.
