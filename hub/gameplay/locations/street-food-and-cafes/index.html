How It Works

Leonida runs on cheap food. Hot dog carts on the beach, juice huts on the boardwalk, Cuban counters in the city and roadside BBQ out in the swamp. Walk up, buy, and it goes in your pocket for later.

Look for the heart. Only food with a heart icon heals. A pink heart gets you back to full, an orange heart pushes you past it for a while. The rest gives a short kick to stamina or focus. You can eat anywhere, at the wheel or in the middle of a firefight, but chewing won't stop a bullet.

Street stands show as a white food cart once the fog clears, cafes and takeouts as a blue cutlery icon. Map > Food & Drink keeps count of both. Fast food joints, convenience stores and bars sell food too.

Tips

  • Check for the heart before you buy. Food without one tops up stamina or focus, not health.
  • Orange hearts take you past your normal maximum for a while. Eat one before a fight, not after.
  • You can eat while driving or shooting, but a burger is not armor. Get out of the line of fire first.

Frequently Asked

Can you eat in GTA 6?
Yes. Buy food at street stands, cafes and takeouts, fast food joints and convenience stores, then eat it from your inventory, even on the move.
How do you heal with food in GTA 6?
Pick items with a heart icon. A pink heart heals you up to your normal maximum. An orange heart pushes you past it for a while.
How many street food vendors are in GTA 6?
The map counter tracks 118 street food stands and 87 cafes and takeouts. A few more spots are marked on the map but don't count toward it.
What does food do in GTA 6?
Heart items restore health, and food in general gives a short boost to health, stamina or focus. It never raises your permanent maximum.
