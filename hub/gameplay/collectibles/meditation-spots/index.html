How It Works

Leonida runs loud. These spots are where you turn it down. Ten quiet places, from a sandbar off Capri to a stone circle on the top of Mount Kalaga, each one marked with a purple icon once you get close. Sit down, breathe, and the counter goes up.

It starts at home in Chapter 1. One morning Lucia says she is going to meditate and steps out onto the balcony. Follow her and accept, and Jason learns how. From Chapter 2 you can switch between the two of them, and that is when the outdoor spots pay double: each one counts once as Jason and once as Lucia.

The last spot stays hidden until every other meditation is done. Then it shows up on the map at the summit of Mount Kalaga, and the two of them do that one together.

Tips

  • Spots stay off the map until you get close. Then a purple icon appears, and the map filter Exploration > Meditate Around Leonida shows the ones you have found.
  • Every outdoor spot counts twice: once as Jason, once as Lucia. Do it, switch characters, do it again before you leave.
  • Swamp and Worn Stone sit close together in Kelly County. Park the boat on the northwest shore of the swamp and run to the camp.
  • Water Wheel and Jutting Cliff are a short drive apart in Lummox County. Chain them.
  • The summit spot is the finale: Jason and Lucia meditate there together, and it counts once.

Missables

  • The meditation at Jason's house is only offered in Chapter 1. It shows up one morning when Lucia says she is going to meditate. Accept it when it comes.
  • 100% only needs 15 of 18, so missing it never blocks completion. For the trophy, do it in Chapter 1 to be safe.

Frequently Asked

How many Meditation Spots are in GTA 6?
Ten spots hold 18 meditations. Eight outdoor spots count twice (once as Jason, once as Lucia), the Chapter 1 balcony counts once and the summit of Mount Kalaga counts once.
Where is the last Meditation Spot in GTA 6?
In the stone circle at the top of Mount Kalaga in Lummox County. It only appears on the map after every other meditation is done.
Is the first meditation missable?
Yes. The meditation at Jason's house is only offered during Chapter 1. 100% needs just 15 of 18, so it never blocks completion, but do it in Chapter 1 if you want the trophy without doubt.
What trophy do Meditation Spots unlock?
Positive Mental Attitude, for meditating at every spot across Leonida.
How many meditations do you need for 100% in GTA 6?
Any 15 of the 18.
