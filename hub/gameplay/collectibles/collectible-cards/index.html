How It Works

Somebody in Leonida collects cards and keeps losing them. They turn up on microwaves in gas station break rooms, pinned to office corkboards, on garage shelves, on dugout benches and at the top of radio towers. Walk up, grab it and it lands in Progress > Collections > Collectible Cards.

There are 480 of them across 18 sets. Ten are ball clubs: the Vice City Flamingos, Narcos, Manatees and Club De Futbol, the Liberty City Swingers, Penetrators and Wrath, and the Los Santos Corkers, Panic and Pounders. Then come Backyard Wrestling, America's Next Top Hooker, three dancer sets from Delights, Jack of Hearts and Nine1Nine, and three comic runs: The Legends of Ratface, Right Wing: America's Hero and the Ed Comics Universe. Every color version of a player counts as its own card.

Cards are money too. Any pawn shop pays for them, and selling one does not undo it in your collection. Three full sets earn a trophy, fifteen clear the 100% line, and finishing the Vice City Flamingos unlocks the Bubblegum screen filter for after the story.

Tips

  • Every color is its own card. Bronze, Silver, Gold, Light Blue and Pink versions of the same player all count separately, so keep looking after the first one.
  • Garages, break rooms, back offices and balconies hide most of them. On the map, the darker blue buildings are the ones you can walk into.
  • Sell freely. A card you sell to a pawn shop stays ticked off in its set.
  • Three full sets earn the trophy. Fifteen full sets clear the line on the 100% list, so three sets can be skipped entirely.
  • The dancer sets are tied to the clubs: Delights, Jack of Hearts and Nine1Nine each have their own deck.

Missables

  • None. Cards stay out in the world after the story, and nothing on the 100% list can be locked out.

Frequently Asked

How many collectible cards are in GTA 6?
480, split over 18 sets. The sets run from 12 cards (Backyard Wrestling) to 36 (the comic sets and two of the dancer sets).
How many card sets do you need for 100% in GTA 6?
15 of the 18 sets, each one complete. Three full sets already earn the trophy Don't Touch My Deck.
What do you get for the Vice City Flamingos cards in GTA 6?
All 24 Vice City Flamingos cards unlock the Bubblegum screen filter cheat. Like every cheat, it only works after the story.
Can you sell collectible cards in GTA 6?
Yes. Pawn shops buy them, and a card you have sold still counts toward its set and toward 100%.
Are collectible cards missable in GTA 6?
No. You can pick them up in free roam at any point, before or after the story.
