Lucia Caminos

Liberty City born. Raised to fight, and she never stopped. Fresh out of Leonida Penitentiary on an ankle monitor, with every reason to stay out.

Dossier

Lucia Caminos was born in Liberty City and raised to fight. Her father started the lessons as soon as she could stand, and life has been swinging at her ever since. Standing up for her family is what put her inside Leonida Penitentiary. Luck, more than anything, is what gets her out.

She comes out with an ankle monitor and a plan, and she means to stick to the plan. Smart moves only from here. She wants the good life her mother always talked about, and a life with Jason Duval might be the road to it. Leonida keeps raising the price.

In a fight she is the technical one: kickboxing, MMA, blocks and counters where Jason just swings. The same Focus that slows a firefight for him works for her, and everything she does lands on her own Criminal Profile, never his. Her Strength, Cardio and Focus are hers to keep up. Skip the gym and she loses it.

She does not sit still when you are not playing her. Switch to Jason and Lucia carries on with her own day, and she may be across town by the time you switch back.

Story Arc

The story starts with Lucia, behind a cell door, and she carries more of it than anyone gives her credit for: a prologue, five chapters, and a reason to keep going that the game holds back until the end of the opening. Every reveal below stays shut until you open it.

Spoiler: Prologue Tap to reveal

Lucia is inside, talking her way into supervised release with her counselor, Stefanie. A flashback shows how she got there: broke and out of options, she robs a payday loan store across the street from the café where she has been sitting.

What the camera hides until her arrest: she left her baby son, Tomás, at that café. Child services take him. Getting him back is the point of everything she does from here.

Spoiler: Chapter 1 Tap to reveal

Out on an ankle monitor and tied to Jason's address in the Keys, she still talks her way onto his work, and tells him the boy is his. When the pickup at the Grassrivers visitor center turns into a police ambush, she keeps a wounded Jason alive, then breaks him out of a Vice City hospital with the police and worse closing in.

Spoiler: Chapter 2 Tap to reveal

Lucia carries this chapter. Hiding Jason at the Starlet Motel in Port Gellhorn, she finds the rent, the painkillers and a car, builds her own little network in town and starts boosting cars. Once Jason is up, the two of them rob their way to the move to Vice City.

Spoiler: Chapter 3 Tap to reveal

In Vice City she is the first through Raul Bautista's tests, and back in her mother's orbit. Mia sells real estate now, and one of her deals drags Lucia into a fight with the local gang next door. The chapter ends on Raul's job at Sinfrontera National Bank.

Spoiler: Chapter 4 Tap to reveal

From the Ocean Beach suite she goes undercover with the crew planning an airport job. Then Raul is gone, handed to the dirty cop Aldo Méndez by Valentina, someone they trusted.

Spoiler: Chapter 5 Tap to reveal

The last plan runs through Giorgio Gallo, the shipping magnate the bag belonged to all along. He promises the pair a pardon, then sells them out. There is more than one ending, and the finale can be run again for another outcome.

Frequently Asked

Who is Lucia Caminos in GTA 6?
One of the two playable leads. Born in Liberty City and raised to fight by her father, she lands in Leonida Penitentiary for standing up for her family and walks out wanting the good life her mother always talked about. She runs with Jason Duval.
Who voices Lucia Caminos?
Manni L. Perez, named in Rockstar's launch-day credits. The rest of the cast is in the voice actors FAQ.
Is Lucia pregnant in GTA 6?
No. She is in the fight from the first mission to the last. Where the theory came from is in the full answer.
How does Lucia fight?
Kickboxing and MMA. Blocks, counters and kicks, where Jason just swings. She shares Focus with him, but her Strength, Cardio, Focus and Criminal Profile are her own.
Why does Lucia wear an ankle monitor?
She starts the story on supervised release from prison, and the monitor comes with it. The woman who signs off on it is her counselor, Stefanie.
Who is after Jason and Lucia?
The setup is all you get going in: an easy score goes wrong and drops them into a conspiracy that runs across Leonida. The names sit behind the reveal in the Story Arc above and in the antagonist FAQ.
